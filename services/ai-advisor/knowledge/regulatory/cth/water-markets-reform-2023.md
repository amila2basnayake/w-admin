# Water Amendment (Restoring Our Rivers) Act 2023

> **FAST-MOVING — ENTIRE DOC.** The Act's provisions commenced in stages (Dec 2023 through
> July 2026) and the deadlines it set are approaching; the 2026 Basin Plan Review may change
> settings again. Verify every date and obligation against current DCCEEW / ACCC / BOM material.
> Pre-December-2023 sources are stale on all points below (old 450 GL deadline, buyback cap,
> "insider trading rules are only a recommendation").

## What it is

Commenced **7 December 2023**. Amended the Water Act 2007 (Cth) and Basin Plan 2012 to deliver the
Basin Plan with "more options, more time, more funding and more accountability". Two halves:
Basin Plan delivery changes, and a water markets integrity package implementing the ACCC's 2021
water markets inquiry recommendations.

## Basin Plan delivery changes

- **450 GL environmental water target**: deadline moved to **31 December 2027** (from
  30 June 2024). Recovery options broadened beyond efficiency measures to include on-farm and
  off-farm infrastructure projects, **voluntary water purchases (buybacks)**, and land-and-water
  packages.
- **SDL adjustment (supply/constraint) projects**: extended to **31 December 2026**.
- **1,500 GL buyback cap REPEALED**: the statutory limit on Commonwealth surface-water entitlement
  purchases is gone. Any advice that the cap still applies is stale — this changed December 2023.

## Water markets integrity package (COMMENCED 1 July 2026)

Both conduct prohibitions and announcement obligations are **now in force** — not proposals.

### Conduct prohibitions
- **Insider trading**: Basin-wide prohibition on trading water rights while aware of non-public
  market-sensitive information. Applies to **all market participants**.
- **Market manipulation**: new bans on manipulating water market prices.
- **Enforcement: ACCC.** The ACCC also retains its long-standing Water Act roles (advice on Basin
  Plan trading rules; monitoring regulated water charges under ss 94 and 99).

### Announcement obligations
- Commonwealth and Basin **state government agencies** and **irrigation infrastructure operators
  (IIOs)** must announce water markets decisions that may materially affect trading behaviour
  (e.g. allocation announcements). ACCC oversees compliance.
- **Publication channel**: announcements are submitted through the **Bureau of Meteorology's water
  data hub** and published centrally on BOM's water markets webpages.

### Data and records
- BOM gained new water markets data functions: it develops **Water Markets Data Standards** and
  collects and publishes Basin water markets information, including trades and offers to trade.
- **Record-keeping: 5 years** for water markets records (verify — under review; retention period
  not confirmed in the mined sources).

## Advisory notes

- Sequence matters when dating advice: Act commenced 7 Dec 2023; conduct rules and announcement
  obligations commenced 1 July 2026. A source from 2024-25 may correctly describe the rules as
  "legislated but not yet commenced" — that is now out of date.
- The 450 GL and supply-project deadlines interact with the SDL adjustment mechanism (see the
  Basin Plan SDL doc); the 2026 Basin Plan Review is running and may recommend further change.
