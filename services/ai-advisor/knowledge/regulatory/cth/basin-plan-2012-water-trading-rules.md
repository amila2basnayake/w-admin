# Basin Plan 2012 — Chapter 12 water trading rules

Chapter 12 (**ss 12.01-12.52**) sets the Basin-wide water trading rules. It is organised into Parts,
each with its own "Object of this Part" section (12.31, 12.36, 12.40). Section headings below are
verbatim from the instrument; the operative sub-text of each section is not quoted in full (see
gaps).

**Tagged entitlements — 2024 change:** the exemption for grandfathered tagged water access
entitlements (former s 12.23(2)) was **removed effective 1 Jul 2024**. All tagged entitlements are
now uniformly subject to the restrictions applying to water allocation trade — pre-2014 "grandfathered"
tags no longer bypass allocation trade restrictions (e.g. IVT limits).

**Water Act regime from 1 Jul 2026:** the Basin Plan insider-trading rules (ss 12.50-12.52,
IGWC-enforced) applied only until the Water Act prohibitions commenced. From 1 Jul 2026 the
operative regime is the ACCC-enforced Water Act prohibitions — insider trading and market
manipulation (ss 101JG, 101JH, 101JJ, 101JK) and water-markets-decision announcement obligations.
Trade-form accuracy and 5-year record-keeping duties (Water Act ss 135M/135N, IGWC-enforced) have
applied since 1 Jul 2024. See `cth-accc-water-market-charge-rules`.

## Key takeaways for interpreting a trade

- Trade in water access rights is presumptively **free**; restrictions are the exception and must
  fall within the allowable grounds (physical/hydrological/environmental, s 12.18; State
  water-management-law breaches, s 12.27).
- **Exchange rates** are prohibited in regulated systems (s 12.21) except where the Authority
  permits them in limited circumstances (s 12.22).
- Carried-over water allocation may be traded (s 12.11) and access to carryover cannot be denied to
  traded rights (s 12.12).
- Tagged entitlements no longer escape allocation trade restrictions (former s 12.23(2) exemption
  removed 1 Jul 2024).
- Trade prices must be **reported** (s 12.48). Insider-trading rules moved: the Basin Plan
  s 12.51 restriction applied until 1 Jul 2026; the operative bans are now the ACCC-enforced
  Water Act insider-trading and manipulation prohibitions.

- Section **headings** are verbatim/high-confidence; the full operative sub-text of each section was

- Definitions of "approval authority" and "tagged water access entitlement" were not fetched.
