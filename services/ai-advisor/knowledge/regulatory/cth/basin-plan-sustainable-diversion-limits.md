# Basin Plan 2012 — sustainable diversion limits (SDLs)

## Concept

A **sustainable diversion limit (SDL)** is the long-term average annual amount of water that can be
taken from specified water resources of the Murray-Darling Basin while maintaining environmental
objectives and ecosystem integrity. Surface water and groundwater are managed as **separate SDL
resource units**: **29 surface-water** and **80 groundwater** units (MDBA).

## Where the limits sit in the Plan

| Provision | Content |
|---|---|
| **Chapter 6** ("Water that can be taken", ss 6.01-6.14) | The long-term average SDL framework; identifies SDL resource units. |
| **Schedule 2** (Column 1) | Lists the 29 **surface-water** SDL resource units. |
| **Schedule 4** (Column 1) | Lists the 80 **groundwater** SDL resource units. |
| **s 6.04(1)** | The long-term average SDLs took effect **1 July 2019**; SDL compliance runs from that date. |
| **s 6.07** | The temporary diversion provision for each SDL resource unit is **zero**. |

## SDL adjustment mechanism (SDLAM, Chapter 7)

SDLs can be adjusted via **supply measures** (offset the need to recover water, up to a 605 GL/y
supply contribution) and **efficiency measures** (the 450 GL/y additional environmental water).
Initial adjustments were determined in 2017. The **Water Amendment (Restoring Our Rivers) Act 2023**
reset the timeframes:

| Milestone | Deadline (current) |
|---|---|
| SDLAM supply-measure projects operational | **31 Dec 2026** (extended from 30 Jun 2024) |
| SDLAM reconciliation (final supply contribution vs the 605 GL/y offset) | By **31 Dec 2026**; MDBA to finalise late 2026 |
| 450 GL/y efficiency-measure water recovery | **31 Dec 2027** |

Status (Basin Officials Committee, Jun 2026): of 39 notified SDLAM projects, 13 complete, a further
10 expected on time, and 14 withdrawn as unable to operate by the deadline — a supply shortfall
against 605 GL/y is expected at reconciliation. Verify against current MDBA announcements; the
reconciliation outcome will change effective SDLs.

## 2026 Basin Plan Review

The MDBA's 2026 Basin Plan Review (final report due Dec 2026) has released an initial SDL
assessment; SDL settings may change following the review. Verify against MDBA announcements.
