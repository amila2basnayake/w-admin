# Water Act 2007 (Cth) — roles and framework

The Water Act 2007 (Cth) (Act No. 137 of 2007; current compilation C2026C00270, 26 Jun 2026)
establishes the Commonwealth framework for managing the water resources of the Murray-Darling
Basin. Administered by the Department of Climate Change, Energy, the Environment and Water.

It was substantially amended by the **Water Amendment (Restoring Our Rivers) Act 2023** (No. 111 of
2023, assent 7 Dec 2023): extended Basin Plan water-recovery timeframes, re-enabled voluntary water
purchasing, and created the water-markets conduct and information regimes below. Descriptions of the
Act predating December 2023 are stale.

## Key roles

| Body | Part | Core functions |
|---|---|---|
| **Murray-Darling Basin Authority (MDBA)** | Part 9 | Prepare and oversee the Basin Plan; coordinate/monitor environmental water delivery; manage water information. |
| **Basin States** (NSW, Vic, SA, Qld, ACT) | Part 2, Division 2 | Develop water resource plans, accredited by the MDBA or prepared/adopted by the Minister. |
| **Commonwealth Environmental Water Holder (CEWH)** | Part 6 | Holds/manages Commonwealth environmental water; Environmental Water Holdings Special Account. |
| **ACCC** | Part 4; Part 5 | Water charge rules (Part 4 Div 1) and water market rules (Part 4 Div 2); from 2025-26 also the intermediaries code, trust accounting and market-conduct prohibitions (see below). |
| **Inspector-General of Water Compliance (IGWC)** | — (role created by 2021 amendments) | Independent Basin compliance/enforcement regulator since Aug 2021 (took over MDBA's compliance functions); enforces trade-form accuracy and record-keeping (ss 135M/135N). |
| **Bureau of Meteorology (BOM)** | Part 7 | Water information functions (collects/publishes water information, National Water Account); now also **water markets data custodian** under the information reforms. |

The Basin Plan 2012 is the primary instrument made under this Act (see `cth-basin-plan-2012-overview`).

## Water-markets conduct regime (2023 reforms, ACCC-enforced)

| Element | Where | Commenced |
|---|---|---|
| Water Markets Intermediaries Code (brokers, exchanges) | Part 5, Water Regulations 2008 (code authorised by Water Act Part 5) | Phased: 1 Jul 2025 and 1 Oct 2025 |
| Statutory trust accounting for intermediaries | Part 5, Water Act 2007 | Phased with the code (1 Jul 2025 / 1 Oct 2025) |
| Insider trading and market manipulation prohibitions | Water Act ss 101JG, 101JH, 101JJ, 101JK | 1 Jul 2026 (enforcement moved from IGWC to ACCC) |
| Water-markets-decision announcement obligations | 2023-reform instruments | 1 Jul 2026 |

## Water markets information (Part 7A)

| Obligation | Sections | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Trade-form accuracy; 5-year record keeping | ss 135M, 135N | In force since 1 Jul 2024; IGWC-enforced. |
| Data-provider pre/post-trade reporting to BOM | ss 135F, 135H | DEFERRED — commences 1 Jul 2027. |
| Water Markets Data Standards 2026 (F2026L00890); Water Amendment (Water Markets Information) Regulations 2026 (F2026L00833) | — | Commenced 1 Jul 2026. |

## Notes

- The ACCC does not run the allocation/entitlement markets or resolve individual trade disputes; it
  enforces the rules above.
- Exact section numbers within Parts 2, 4, 6 and 9 are not enumerated here; the Part attributions
  are per the compilation and ACCC/IGWC sources.
