# ACCC water market rules, water charge rules and market-conduct regime

All rules below are made under the Water Act 2007 (Cth) and apply in the Murray-Darling Basin. The
ACCC regulates infrastructure charging, transformation, and (since 2025-26) intermediary and market
conduct — it does **not** run the allocation/entitlement markets or resolve individual disputes.

## Water Market Rules 2009

In force unchanged (F2009L02424; last compiled 2012).

- Protect irrigators who want to permanently **"transform"** their irrigation right into a statutory
  water access entitlement — held independently, tradeable, and free of trade restrictions imposed
  by an irrigation infrastructure operator (IIO).
- Apply to **irrigation infrastructure operators** in the Murray-Darling Basin.
- **Rule 14** sets the timeframe within which operators must process transformation applications.

## Water Charge Rules 2010

The operative instrument is the **Water Charge Rules 2010** (series F2011L00058; current compilation
**F2020C00877** of 1 Jul 2020). The formerly separate Water Charge (Termination Fees) Rules 2009 and
Water Charge (Infrastructure) Rules 2010 were consolidated into it by the 2019 amendment rules,
effective **1 Jul 2020**, following the ACCC's 2016 review (the ACCC describes the 2019 amendment as
combining the previous charge rules, including the former Planning and Management Information rules,
into the single instrument). **No amendments 2024-2026.**

- Provide **price transparency** for infrastructure and related services in the Basin
  (schedule-of-charges disclosure; pass-through / network / ancillary charges).
- **Termination fee cap:** the maximum general termination fee = **10 times** the fixed volumetric
  access charges for the rights being terminated (per unit, per financial year). Where trading is
  prohibited, the cap = the fixed volumetric charge amount (not multiplied). Any additional
  termination fee applies only if ACCC-approved.

## Market-conduct regime (Water Amendment (Restoring Our Rivers) Act 2023)

Stems from the ACCC's 2021 Murray-Darling Basin water markets inquiry and the Government's "Water
market reform: final roadmap" (2022), legislated by the 2023 Act. All ACCC-enforced:

| Element | Where | Commenced |
|---|---|---|
| **Water Markets Intermediaries Code** — mandatory conduct code for brokers, exchanges and other intermediaries | Part 5, Water Regulations 2008 | Phased: **1 Jul 2025** and **1 Oct 2025** |
| **Statutory trust accounting** for client water/funds held by intermediaries | Part 5, Water Act 2007 | Phased with the code (1 Jul 2025 / 1 Oct 2025) |
| **Insider trading + market manipulation prohibitions** | Water Act ss 101JG, 101JH, 101JJ, 101JK | **1 Jul 2026** (enforcement moved from the IGWC to the ACCC) |
| **Water-markets-decision announcement obligations** | 2023-reform instruments | **1 Jul 2026** |

## Water markets information (BOM data custodian; IGWC-enforced record duties)

| Obligation | Sections / instrument | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Trade-form accuracy; 5-year record keeping | Water Act ss 135M, 135N | In force since **1 Jul 2024**; **IGWC**-enforced (not ACCC). |
| Data-provider pre/post-trade reporting to BOM | Water Act ss 135F, 135H | **Deferred to 1 Jul 2027.** |
| Water Markets Data Standards 2026 (F2026L00890); Water Amendment (Water Markets Information) Regulations 2026 (F2026L00833) | — | Commenced **1 Jul 2026**; BOM is the water markets data custodian. |

## Practical notes for brokers

- From 1 Jul 2025/1 Oct 2025 intermediaries must comply with the code and trust-accounting duties;
  from 1 Jul 2026 the insider-trading and manipulation bans apply to **all** market participants.
- Termination-fee questions: apply the 10x fixed-volumetric cap under the Water Charge Rules 2010.
- Transformation questions: Water Market Rules 2009, rule 14 timeframes.
