# Basin Plan 2012 — overview

The **Basin Plan 2012** (registration **F2012L02240**; current compilation **F2024C00760**,
Compilation No. 10, in force from 1 Jul 2024) is a legislative instrument authorised by the Water
Act 2007 (Cth). It sets Basin-wide limits and rules for managing the water resources of the
Murray-Darling Basin.

It was amended by the **Water Amendment (Restoring Our Rivers) Act 2023** — pre-2023 descriptions of
the Plan's timeframes are stale (see below).

## Structure (chapters relevant to trading and allocation)

| Chapter | Subject |
|---|---|
| **Chapter 6** (ss 6.01-6.14) | "Water that can be taken" — the long-term average **sustainable diversion limit (SDL)** framework; identifies SDL resource units. See `cth-basin-plan-sustainable-diversion-limits`. |
| **Chapter 7** | Adjustment of SDLs via supply and efficiency measures (SDLAM). |
| **Chapter 12** (ss 12.01-12.52) | **Water trading rules** — the core market rules. See `cth-basin-plan-2012-water-trading-rules`. |

## SDL concept (summary)

An SDL is the long-term average annual amount of water that can be taken from specified water
resources while maintaining environmental objectives. Surface water and groundwater are managed as
**separate SDL resource units**: **29 surface-water** units (Schedule 2, Column 1) and **80
groundwater** units (Schedule 4, Column 1) (MDBA). The long-term average SDLs took effect **1 July
2019** (s 6.04(1)). Fuller detail in `cth-basin-plan-sustainable-diversion-limits`.

## Restoring Our Rivers changes (2023 Act) — current timeframes

- **450 GL/y additional environmental water**: recovery deadline extended to **31 Dec 2027**. As of
  mid-2026 about **169.8 GL** recovered — verify against the current DCCEEW progress figures.
- **Voluntary water purchasing (buybacks) re-enabled** (purchases were effectively paused 2015-2023;
  re-enabled by the 2023 Act). Southern-Basin purchase limit 300 GL; a northern-Basin EOI round
  (Mar-Apr 2026) was under evaluation as of Jul 2026 — verify against current tender announcements.
- **SDLAM supply-measure projects**: must be operational by **31 Dec 2026**; reconciliation by late
  2026.

## 2026 Basin Plan Review

The MDBA's statutory review of the Basin Plan is underway: public consultation closed **1 May 2026**
(~2,500 submissions); an initial SDL assessment has been released; final report to governments due
**Dec 2026**. Basin Plan settings (including SDLs) may change as a result — verify current settings
against MDBA announcements.
