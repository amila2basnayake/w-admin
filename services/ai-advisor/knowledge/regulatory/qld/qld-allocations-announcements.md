# QLD — supplemented vs unsupplemented water and announced allocations

Queensland's model differs structurally from the southern-Basin states: there is no single statewide
1 July percentage announcement; availability is announced per scheme or per water source.

## Supplemented vs unsupplemented

| Category | Meaning | Who announces availability |
|---|---|---|
| **Supplemented** | Water supplied from infrastructure (dams, weirs) owned/operated by a **Resource Operations Licence (ROL)** holder — e.g. **Sunwater** or **Seqwater**. | The scheme operator. Sunwater publishes per-scheme announced allocations at sunwater.com.au/customer/announced-allocations/, set at the start of each scheme's water year and updated as availability improves. |
| **Unsupplemented** | Water not reliant on stored/distributed infrastructure (e.g. river harvesting), managed by the **Department of Local Government, Water and Volunteers**. Subject to **announced allocations** and/or **annual announced limits**. | The department notifies holders (SMS/email/mail) **at the start of their water year**, and again during the year in drought/low-availability conditions. |

- **Announced entitlements** are the parallel concept for unsupplemented water **licences**.
- A **water management protocol** sets water-sharing and permanent-trade rules within a plan area;
  schemes run under a ROL and operations manual.
- Water-year start dates are **scheme-specific** (Queensland does not use a uniform 1 July across
  all schemes). **Verify the relevant scheme's current announcement.**

## Temporary trade (seasonal water assignments)

- Queensland temporary trades are **seasonal water assignments** — assignment of part/all of a
  water year's allocated water to another party.
- In **supplemented** schemes, assignments require the **ROL holder's consent** (Sunwater/Seqwater)
  under the scheme's **operations manual** rules.
- **Sunwater** trades seasonal allocations via its online **Water Exchange** platform.
- Unsupplemented assignments follow the plan's water management protocol and are handled by the
  department.

## Murray-Darling Basin schemes

| Plan | Instrument | Schemes |
|---|---|---|
| Border Rivers and Moonie | **Water Plan (Border Rivers and Moonie) 2019** (Basin WRP accredited Sept 2019) | Border Rivers WSS (department-operated); **Macintyre Brook WSS (Sunwater ROL)**; Callandoon; Yambocully. Sits alongside the NSW-Queensland Border Rivers Intergovernmental Agreement 2008. |
| Condamine and Balonne | **Water Plan (Condamine and Balonne) 2019** (accredited Sept 2019) | **St George WSS (Sunwater ROL)**; St George Channel Scheme (Mallawa Irrigation, distribution operations licence); Sunwater also holds ROLs for Chinchilla Weir and Upper Condamine WSS. |

The Condamine and Balonne plan area also governs **unsupplemented groundwater**, including the
**Upper Condamine Alluvium (Central Condamine Alluvium)** unit (sub-areas and zones, 46,221 ML
extraction ceiling, permanent trade among holders) — see `qld-central-condamine-alluvium-groundwater`.

Queensland's Basin catchments also include the **Warrego-Paroo-Nebine** (no supplemented trading
scheme). The Commonwealth Basin conduct instruments (Water Markets Intermediaries Code;
insider-trading/market-manipulation prohibitions from 1 July 2026) apply in Queensland **only to
these MDB water resources** — see `qld-water-act-2000-basics`.

## Interstate trade (QLD-NSW Border Rivers)

- Interstate trade operates in the **Border Rivers catchment** across the NSW-Qld border.
- The traded water **remains registered in, and subject to the rules of, the state of origin**.
- **Permanent interstate dealings do not require prior approval by the state water agency** (they
  are recorded on the register); **temporary trades** assign water accruing to an account in one
  state to be taken in the other during that water year (supplemented temporary transfers move via
  a holding account managed by the origin-state water service provider).
- Deal with the agency/service provider in the state where the trade **originates**.
