# QLD — Water Act 2000 framework basics

The **Water Act 2000 (Qld)** (Act No. 34 of 2000) is Queensland's water-management framework. A
**water plan** applies to part of the State and advances sustainable management of its water.

- Administering department (since the October 2024 machinery-of-government change): **Department of
  Local Government, Water and Volunteers**. (Water previously sat with the Department of Regional
  Development, Manufacturing and Water — RDMW no longer exists.)
- The in-force subordinate legislation is the **Water Regulation 2016 (Qld)** (SL 216 of 2016).
  Dealing rules for water allocations sit in the Water Regulation 2016 plus plan-level **water
  management protocols** (permanent-trade rules) and **operations manuals** (seasonal-assignment
  rules).

## Authorisation types

An authorisation to take water may be a **water allocation, water licence, or water permit** (water
allocations are listed among State authorisations at **s 27(2)(b)(i)**):

| Type | Character | Tradability |
|---|---|---|
| **Water allocation** | Authorises taking a set volume from a watercourse/aquifer; has its **own registrable title separate from land**, held as **personal property**. Created by converting a licence/interim allocation via a **water entitlement notice**. | **Bought, sold and leased** on the water market. |
| **Water licence** | Authority to take water and/or interfere with water; **generally attaches to the land** on which the water is used. | Not freely tradable independent of land (may be converted to an allocation). |
| **Water permit** | For temporary activities with a foreseeable end date. | Cannot be relocated, traded, amended, renewed or suspended. |

## Water Allocations Register (WAR)

- Central register recording **ownership, tenancy and encumbrances** (e.g. mortgages) of water
  allocations — analogous to the land register. **Titles Queensland** handles registration, using
  the same forms as land transfers.
- Permanent dealings split two ways:
  - **Registration only**: transfers (sales) and leases are registered on the WAR without prior
    departmental approval.
  - **Departmental approval first**: dealings that change the allocation itself — **change,
    subdivision, amalgamation** — need approval from the Department of Local Government, Water and
    Volunteers before registration.

## Murray-Darling relevance

Queensland's MDB catchments are the **Condamine-Balonne, Border Rivers, Moonie and
Warrego-Paroo-Nebine**. The trading catchments are governed by **Water Plan (Border Rivers and
Moonie) 2019** and **Water Plan (Condamine and Balonne) 2019** (both accredited as Basin water
resource plans in September 2019) — see `qld-allocations-announcements`.

Queensland is otherwise **outside the scope of the Commonwealth Basin conduct instruments**: the
**Water Markets Intermediaries Code** (in full operation since 1 October 2025) and the
**insider-trading and market-manipulation prohibitions** (commenced 1 July 2026, ACCC-enforced)
apply to **MDB water resources only** — i.e. in Queensland only to the Basin catchments above, not
to the rest of the State.
