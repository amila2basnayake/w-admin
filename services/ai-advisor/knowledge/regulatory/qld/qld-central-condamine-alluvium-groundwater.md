# QLD — Central Condamine Alluvium groundwater (Condamine and Balonne)

Water in the **Upper Condamine Alluvium (Central Condamine Alluvium) underground water unit** sits in Queensland's **Condamine and Balonne** water plan area. It is **unsupplemented groundwater**: there is no dam or scheme operator releasing water: the holder pumps from the aquifer under a licence, and take is **metered** and must stay within the licensed nominal entitlement and conditions. Availability is driven by aquifer resource condition, not a stored-water "allocation %" like the southern Murray systems.

## The two instruments that set the rules

| Instrument | What it governs |
|---|---|
| **Water Plan (Condamine and Balonne) 2019** (SL 2019 No. 11) | Plan objectives, the underground water management areas, sub-areas and zones, the extraction limit, and who may hold/trade |
| **Condamine and Balonne Water Management Protocol (Feb 2019)** | Operational detail: water-sharing rules for the alluvium, seasonal water assignment rules, and the water-allocation dealing (trade) rules that apply sub-area by sub-area |

Both sit under the **Water Act 2000 (Qld)**. Dealing applications are processed under the **Water Regulation 2016**, and ownership/mortgages are recorded on the **Water Allocations Register**. Queensland's Department of Regional Development, Manufacturing and Water (**RDMW**) administers them.

## Verified figures and provisions (Water Plan 2019)

- **Extraction ceiling — 46,221 ML.** The total volume of nominal entitlements for all water licences to take underground water from the Upper Condamine Alluvium (Central Condamine Alluvium) unit, **other than licences held by the CEWH, must not exceed 46,221 ML** — **s 59(2)(b)(i)**.
- **Minimum licence size — 5 ML.** An individual licence's nominal entitlement would **not be less than 5 ML** — **s 59(2)(b)(iii)**.
- **Sub-areas and zones are the management/trading unit.** The plan divides the aquifer into **underground water sub-areas** (Schedule 5, referenced in s 11) and **underground water zones** (Schedule 6, s 13), with **trading zones** for water allocations set under **s 14**. Sub-area 2 is one of these sub-areas.
- **Sharing and dealing rules live in the protocol.** The protocol must state the **water-sharing rules** for licences taking water in this unit (**s 82(2)(e)**) and the **seasonal water assignment and water-allocation dealing rules** for water not managed under a resource operations licence (**s 82(2)(d)**).

## Trading

- **Permanent trade among entitlement holders is allowed.** The water-sharing rules were amended so Central Condamine Alluvium holders can **permanently trade a water licence among themselves**, rather than only seasonally assigning water each year. The change was made so an **open water market** operated — a precondition for holders' participation in **Commonwealth buybacks** under the Basin Plan (Qld ministerial statement, [72003](https://statements.qld.gov.au/statements/72003)).
- **Sub-areas are where restrictions bite.** The protocol's dealing rules control whether and how water can move **within** a sub-area and **between adjoining** sub-areas. Trade out of a sub-area is where a transfer can be refused even when both parties are willing — check the sub-area dealing rule first.
- Both seller and buyer must be in an **eligible location** under the protocol's dealing rules for a permanent transfer to be approved.

## What is NOT captured here — check the protocol schedules / RDMW

The **precise geographic boundary of each sub-area**, the **specific sub-area-to-sub-area dealing table**, and any **current-year announced-entitlement percentage** for the alluvium sit in the **schedules/appendices of the Condamine and Balonne Water Management Protocol** and are not reproduced above. These are seasonal/operational and must be confirmed against the current protocol and RDMW before relying on them for a specific trade.
