# Allocation announcements compared — NSW, VIC, SA, QLD

All southern-Basin states start their water year on **1 July** (QLD is scheme-specific). Everything
in this table is **seasonal — always verify against the current-season announcement** at the listed
publication point.

| State | Instrument | Who announces | Cadence | Units | Where published |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **NSW** | **Available water determination (AWD)**, WMA 2000 s 59 | The **Minister** (statutory); assessments by **NSW DCCEEW** (the water department — not WaterNSW); results published as **Water Allocation Statements** | Conservative opening AWD **1 July** (2026-27 opening AWDs announced 1 Jul 2026); then normally **monthly** in-season updates (Murray adds a mid-month statement); event-driven supplementary announcements. AWDs can only credit accounts | **ML per unit share** or % (1 ML/share = 100%) | Water Allocation Statements per water source: water.dcceew.nsw.gov.au |
| **VIC** | **Seasonal determination**, Water Act 1989 s 64GA | **Resource Manager** — northern Victoria: Goulburn-Murray Water as **Northern Victoria Resource Manager (NVRM)**; southern: Southern Rural Water | Opening determination **1 July**; then updates on approximately the **1st and 15th of each month, August-May**, until fully allocated. High-reliability shares fill before low-reliability | **% of water share volume** (HRWS and LRWS separately) | waterregister.vic.gov.au seasonal-determinations; nvrm.net.au |
| **SA** | **River Murray Water Allocation Statement** under the River Murray WAP | **Department for Environment and Water (DEW)**, having regard to the MDBA worst-case assessment | Pre-season projections (incl. the ~**15 April** statement that also settles the carryover trigger); opening allocation announced ~**mid-June**; from 1 July, **fortnightly** while below 100% | **% (kL per unit share)** for Class 3 (High Security) | environment.sa.gov.au (statement PDFs on cdn.environment.sa.gov.au) |
| **QLD** | **Announced allocation** (supplemented) / announced allocations-limits and announced entitlements (unsupplemented) | **Scheme operator** (ROL holder — Sunwater, Seqwater) for supplemented schemes; the **Department of Local Government, Water and Volunteers** for unsupplemented (RDMW was abolished Oct 2024) | At the **start of the scheme's water year** (not uniformly 1 July), updated through the year, mainly in drought/low-availability | Scheme-specific | Scheme operator notices (sunwater.com.au announced allocations); Business Queensland; direct holder notification |

## Structural notes

- NSW announces **per licence category** within each water source (priority order: utilities/D&S,
  then high security, then general security, then supplementary) — see
  `nsw-available-water-determinations`.
- Victoria announces **per system** (Murray, Goulburn, Campaspe, Loddon, Broken, Bullarook, Ovens)
  split HRWS/LRWS — see `vic-seasonal-determinations`.
- SA announces a single Class 3 (High Security) percentage for the River Murray — see
  `sa-river-murray-water-allocation-plan`.
- **BOM central reporting (from 1 July 2026):** water-markets decisions — allocation
  announcements, carryover decisions, trade opening/closing — must be announced via, or reported
  to, the **Bureau of Meteorology** for publication on its central water-markets portal
  (wm.bom.gov.au), under the Water Amendment (Water Markets Decisions) Regulations 2026.
- Announcement-integrity overlay: from **1 July 2026** trading on non-public water announcement
  information is prohibited under the **Water Act 2007** insider-trading provisions
  (ACCC-enforced) — see `cross-trading-rules-comparison`.
