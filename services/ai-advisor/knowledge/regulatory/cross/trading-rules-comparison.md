# Trading rules compared — NSW, VIC, SA, QLD and interstate

Basin-wide overlay first: under **Basin Plan 2012 Chapter 12**, trade of water access rights is
presumptively free; restrictions are lawful only on physical/environmental grounds (s 12.18) or for
State water-law breaches (s 12.27); exchange rates are prohibited in regulated systems (s 12.21);
trade prices must be reported (s 12.48). See `cth-basin-plan-2012-water-trading-rules`.

## Market-conduct and enforcement overlay (current from 1 July 2026)

- **Insider trading and market manipulation** in eligible tradeable water rights (MDB) are now
  prohibited under the **Water Act 2007 (ss 101JG, 101JH, 101JJ, 101JK)**, enforced by the
  **ACCC**, from **1 July 2026**. The prior Basin Plan insider-trading rules enforced by the
  Inspector-General of Water Compliance (IGWC) ended on that date.
- The **IGWC retains** Basin Plan / water resource plan compliance and the trade-data obligations
  (trade-form accuracy and 5-year record-keeping, in force since 1 July 2024).
- The **Water Markets Intermediaries Code** (Water Regulations 2008 Pt 5) commenced in phases on
  **1 July 2025** and **1 October 2025**, ACCC-enforced, **MDB water resources only**: brokers and
  other intermediaries must act in good faith, manage conflicts of interest, and hold client money
  in statutory trust/broking accounts.
- From **1 July 2026** water-markets decisions (allocation announcements, carryover, trade
  open/close) must be announced via or reported to the **BOM** (central portal wm.bom.gov.au) —
  see `cross-allocation-announcements-comparison`.

## Approval bodies and headline restrictions

| Jurisdiction | Permanent (entitlement) trade | Temporary (allocation) trade | Signature restrictions |
|---|---|---|---|
| **NSW** | **s 71Q share assignment** under WMA 2000, assessed against the Access Licence Dealing Principles Order 2004 (still in force) + WSP dealing rules; lodged via WaterNSW; recorded on the NSW water register | **s 71T allocation assignment**, same assessment layers; regulated-valley temporary trades processed by WaterNSW | Murrumbidgee->Murray IVT operated 0-100 GL with net trade positive toward the Murray; per-WSP dealing prohibitions (e.g. stressed sources) |
| **VIC** | **Water share transfer** (ownership and/or location) approved and recorded on the Victorian Water Register; transferring a share does not transfer the allocation account | Approved by the **seller's water corporation** if complete and compliant with local rules; permitted between trading zones and interstate | Goulburn/Campaspe/Loddon/Broken -> Murray + interstate: **200 GL IVT limit**, opportunity released via 1 Jul / 15 Oct / 15 Dec announcements; tagged use counts against the same limit; Barmah Narrows net-balance cap |
| **SA** | Administered by **DEW** under the Landscape SA Act 2019 + River Murray WAP Ch 7 | Same (WAP Ch 7); carryover water tradable (Principle 129) | Class 8 (ELMA) and Metropolitan Adelaide pool entitlements **cannot be traded**; Ministerial location/volumetric limits possible (Principle 130) |
| **QLD** | Permanent dealings **registered on the Water Allocations Register** (Titles Queensland); permanent interstate Border Rivers dealings do not require prior state-agency approval | **Seasonal water assignment** under the scheme's operations manual / water management protocol; supplemented interstate temporary trade via an origin-state holding account | Interstate trade limited to the **Border Rivers** connected area (incl. Macintyre Brook); traded water stays under origin-state rules |
| **Interstate (sMDB)** | Requires approval of the water authorities in **both states**, under the MDB Agreement Schedule D protocols (the *Processing Interstate and Intervalley Trade Protocol 2026* replaced the six 2010 protocols from 1 July 2026); tagged trade or transfer — see `cross-interstate-trade-mechanics` | 1:1, both-state processing subject to each state's account limits and IVT rules | **NSW->VIC allocation trade capped at net 200 GL/year or the <50% Murray spill-risk volume, whichever is lesser**; Barmah Narrows balance; IVT open/close status |

## Processing timeframes (rule-level)

- **WaterNSW service standards (COAG KPIs):** 90% of **intrastate** allocation trades in **5
  business days**; **interstate** in **10**. Trades involving SA have been reported at up to **20
  business days** but WaterNSW's published figures are inconsistent — verify against the current
  WaterNSW trade-statistics page before quoting.
- **SA:** River Murray allocation transfers have a **guaranteed processing date of the third
  Friday in June** — applications after that may not be approved before the water year ends
  30 June.
- **NSW end-of-season close:** NSW stops accepting/processing allocation trades shortly before
  30 June each year with **no backdating** (the 2025-26 season closed 16 June 2026) — verify the
  current-season closing date before advising on late-season trades.

## Practical checklist before a cross-border/cross-valley trade

1. Is the relevant **IVT account open** (Goulburn 200 GL; Murrumbidgee 0-100 GL)? See
   `cross-ivt-open-close-mechanics`.
2. Does the trade cross the **Barmah Narrows (Barmah Choke)** downstream? Check the current trade
   balance. See `cross-barmah-choke`.
3. Do the **destination and origin rules** both allow it (WSP dealing rules / Vic zone rules / SA
   WAP Ch 7 / QLD protocol)?
4. Is the client or firm caught by the **conduct overlay** (intermediaries code duties; insider
   trading around allocation announcements)?
5. All of these are **seasonal** — verify open/closed status against the current-season
   announcements (MDBA, Victorian Water Register, WaterNSW).
