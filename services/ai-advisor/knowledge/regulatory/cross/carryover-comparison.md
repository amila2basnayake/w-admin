# Carryover rules compared — NSW, VIC, SA, QLD

Carryover = taking unused water allocation from one water year into the next. Every state does it
differently. All figures are rule-level; **availability and headroom are seasonal — verify against
the current-season announcement** in each state.

| State | Who can carry over | Headline limit | Mechanism | Set by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **NSW** | Varies by valley and licence category (chiefly general security) | Per Water Sharing Plan — e.g. NSW Murray general security: carryover up to **50% of entitlement**, with a **110% of entitlement account limit** (credits from allocation + carryover; allocation trade does not count against it; water above the limit is forgone and re-allocated) | Water allocation account rules per WSP; supplementary/high security generally cannot carry over in most valleys | Each valley's **Water Sharing Plan** (statutory) |
| **VIC** | Water share holders in declared systems | Up to **100% of water share volume**; amounts above 100% go to a **spillable water account**, unusable until a **low-risk-of-spill declaration**; small systems (Broken, Loddon, Bullarook, Werribee) cap carryover + new allocation at 100% | Allocation account + spillable water account | Victorian carryover rules (Water Register / Resource Manager) |
| **SA** | Class 3 (High Security) River Murray entitlement holders only | Max **20% of entitlement** (0.2 kL/unit share); allocation + carryover capped at 100%; excess quarantined | **Drought-triggered**: only offered when the projected minimum opening allocation is **50% or less**, settled in the ~**15 April** statement; 5% storage-loss deduction. Not offered for 2026-27 (projection 62%) — verify against the current-season announcement | River Murray WAP s 5.5.2 (Principles 62-74) |
| **QLD** | Scheme-dependent | No single statewide rule; carryover is a **scheme-level product** in supplemented schemes (operator terms), and unsupplemented arrangements are per water plan | Scheme operator (e.g. Sunwater) carryover products / plan rules | ROL operations manuals / water plans |

## Key structural differences

- **Vic** carryover is a standing feature with a spill-risk overlay; **SA** carryover exists only in
  dry-outlook years (trigger-based); **NSW** carryover is a permanent account rule but its size is
  valley-specific; **QLD** has no uniform concept.
- Basin Plan overlay: a state cannot restrict **trade** of carried-over allocation (s 12.11) nor
  deny carryover access to traded rights (s 12.12) — see `cth-basin-plan-2012-water-trading-rules`.
- Reporting overlay: from **1 July 2026**, carryover decisions (like other water-markets decisions)
  must be announced via or reported to the **Bureau of Meteorology** for central publication
  (wm.bom.gov.au portal).

## Gaps

- QLD per-scheme carryover percentages were not verified.
- NSW figures above are the Murray valley example (MDBA product page + DCCEEW); confirm per-valley
  numbers in the relevant WSP before quoting (see `nsw-carryover-account-limits`).
