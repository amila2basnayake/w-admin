# Corpus coverage manifest

This document states what the regulatory corpus covers so the advisor can treat **absence as a
signal to search the web**, never as licence to answer from memory. If a search returns nothing
and the topic sits in an uncovered cell below, say so, WebSearch, and cite what you find.

## Coverage by jurisdiction

| Jurisdiction | Covered | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Commonwealth / Basin Plan | PARTIAL | Water Act 2007, Basin Plan, SDLs, trading rules, ACCC charge rules. **Now also**: water-markets reform (announcement obligations, insider-trading prohibitions). |
| NSW (Basin) | PARTIAL | WMA essentials, licence categories, AWDs, carryover, s71 dealings, water sharing plans. **Now also**: enforcement & penalties, basic landholder / harvestable rights. |
| NSW (coastal / non-Basin) | NO | Coastal water sharing plans, harvestable rights specifics beyond the state-wide doc — verify by web. |
| VIC | PARTIAL | Water Act 1989, water shares/reliability, carryover/spillable, seasonal determinations, trading zones/IVT. |
| SA | PARTIAL | River Murray WAP, private carryover, trade approvals. Non-Murray SA (groundwater areas, landscape boards) — verify by web. |
| QLD | PARTIAL | Water Act 2000 basics, allocations/announcements. Overland flow, water plans by catchment — verify by web. |
| WA | MINIMAL | RIWI Act essentials doc only — anything beyond it, verify by web. WA is licence-based, fixed-term, NOT MDB-style unbundled. |
| TAS | MINIMAL | WMA 1999 essentials doc only — anything beyond it, verify by web. |
| NT / ACT | NO | Verify by web. |

## Coverage by doctrine area

| Doctrine area | Covered | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Entitlements / allocations / carryover / trading | YES | Core corpus strength (Basin states). |
| Enforcement, penalties, prosecutions | PARTIAL | Per-state enforcement doc (NSW detailed; others summary). Penalty figures are fast-moving — always carry `as_at` and verify if older than ~6 months. |
| Basic landholder / riparian / harvestable rights | PARTIAL | Per-state summary doc. NSW coastal harvestable-rights percentage has CHANGED TWICE since 2022 — never quote it without checking the doc's history section and verifying current. |
| Bore / works approvals | NO | Verify by web (state-specific: works approvals, driller licensing, construction standards). |
| Irrigation infrastructure operators (MIL, MI, CICL, G-MW districts) | PARTIAL | IIO mechanics doc: IIO customers lodge trades and hold delivery entitlements through their operator, not the state register. Operator-specific rules — verify with the operator. |
| Floodplain harvesting | PARTIAL | Covered within NSW docs; valley-by-valley commencement — verify current. |
| Cth water-markets reform (conduct rules, announcements) | YES | Dedicated doc; rules commenced 1 July 2026 — treat as fast-moving. |
| Urban/potable water, dam safety engineering, planning law | NO | Out of corpus scope; answer only the water-rights side and refer the rest. |

## Standing rule

A topic marked NO or MINIMAL is not an invitation to guess. The failure mode this manifest exists
to prevent: declaring a jurisdiction uncovered and then answering its edge cases from memory
anyway. Uncovered = search, cite, and label anything unverifiable.
