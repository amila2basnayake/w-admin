# Barmah Choke / Barmah Narrows trade restriction

The Barmah Narrows (still widely called the **Barmah Choke**) is a natural constriction of the
River Murray between Yarrawonga and the Barmah-Millewa forest, where channel capacity is far lower
than the reaches above and below. It separates Victorian trading **zone 6 (above)** from **zone 7
(below)**. It restricts trade in **two different ways** that must not be conflated.

## Get the terms right first (Water Act 2007, s 4)

- **water access entitlement** — a perpetual/ongoing entitlement to a **share of the water resources
  of a water resource plan area** (in NSW, the access licence **share component**; in Victoria, the
  water share). This is the *permanent* right.
- **water allocation** — the specific volume allocated to entitlements in a water accounting period.
  This is the *seasonal/temporary* water.
- **Where water may be taken is a separate attribute** from the entitlement itself. In NSW an access
  licence has a **share component and an extraction component** (WMA 2000 s 56), and the physical
  location is fixed by the **nominated water supply work** (s 71W). The Choke rules bite on this
  *location of extraction*, not on the share quantum.

## 1. Permanent entitlement — you cannot relocate the point of extraction above→below

- **An entitlement's point of extraction cannot be permanently moved from above to below the Choke**,
  except to *reverse* a prior below→above relocation recorded in the **Barmah Choke entitlement
  record**. Source: **Processing Protocol 2026, cl 11**; in NSW, **WSP 2016 cl 49(1)(a)** (a s 71Q
  assignment is barred where it moves the share to a licence nominating a work downstream of the
  Choke) and **cl 53(a)** (an above-Choke licence cannot re-nominate a below-Choke work).
- **Below→above is not prohibited.** It is recorded in the Barmah Choke entitlement record and can
  offset later above→below back trade (**Adjusting No. 2 Protocol 2026, cl 17-18**). Note the
  grounding: no provision *expressly permits* below→above — it follows from the **absence of any
  prohibition** plus the accounting machinery that assumes and records it, on the Basin Plan
  Chapter 12 presumption that trade is allowed unless a rule restricts it.
- **A permanent share trade that keeps extraction on the same side of the Choke is unaffected** by
  the Choke rules (e.g. above→above, below→below).

## 2. Allocation (temporary) trade — the net-balance / no-net-trade rule

- The MDBA maintains a **Barmah Choke allocation account**, reset to zero each **1 July**;
  below→above transfers **increase** it, above→below transfers **decrease** it, and no approval may
  push it **negative** (Processing Protocol cl 10; Adjusting No. 2 Protocol cl 18(5)).
- **"No net trade rule"**: no *net* above→below allocation trade may occur unless the MDBA determines
  **exceptional circumstances** — defined as when the usual channel-capacity delivery challenges at
  the Choke are **significantly reduced** (Processing Protocol cl 12(2); Annexure item 17).
  **Back trade** (reversing prior below→above volume) is exempt and always available.
- The Authority must announce **by 31 May** each year whether it will even consider an
  exceptional-circumstances determination for the next financial year (cl 13), and publishes a live
  **Barmah Choke balance tool** on its website (Adjusting No. 2 Protocol cl 19).
- In NSW, **WSP 2016 cl 51(a)** mirrors this: an allocation assignment from an above-Choke work to a
  below-Choke work is prohibited unless the Minister is satisfied it will not diminish supply of
  orders already held downstream.

## Distinct: the NSW→VIC 200 GL limit

Separate from the Choke, **allocation trade from NSW to Victoria is capped at a net 200 GL/year, or
the volume that keeps the risk of spill in Victoria's Murray share below 50%, whichever is lesser**
(Victorian Water Register). Do not confuse this with the Choke balance.

## Seasonal balance and openings — always verify the current season

- Under normal operations the allocation balance is **cleared at 30 June** and a fresh opening volume
  **credited on 1 July**, adjusted for Snowy-related savings downstream of the Choke.
- The opening volume varies year to year; recent openings have been released in tranches (e.g. an
  above→below opportunity was released in July 2025). **Verify the current available trade
  opportunity against the MDBA balance page / WaterNSW trade update before advising any
  cross-Choke allocation trade** — it moves within a season.
- NSW and Victoria run a **synchronised Barmah opening** with a fixed submission window and
  randomised processing order; demand routinely exceeds the balance. Check the current opening
  protocol before the event.

## Repealed source note

The **Permissible Transfers between Trading Zones Protocol 2010** and five other 2010 Schedule D
protocols were **repealed from 1 July 2026** by the Processing Protocol 2026 (Schedule 1). Do not
cite the 2010 protocols. Trading-zone definitions and per-zone conditions now sit in **procedures**
made by the Basin Officials Committee.
