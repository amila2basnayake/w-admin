# NSW — available water determinations (AWDs)

## What an AWD is

An **available water determination** is the formal process of allocating water to water access
licence holders: it "results in water accounts of associated water access licences being credited
with a volume of water". Commonly called an "allocation announcement". Statutory basis: **WMA 2000
s 59** (cited in the fetched Murray, Lachlan and Macquarie allocation methodologies).

## Who makes it, per what

- Statutorily the **Minister** makes AWDs for a category/subcategory of access licence; the resource
  assessment behind it is done by the department (DCCEEW water group), and results are published as
  **Water Allocation Statements (WAS)** on the department's website, per water source.
- AWDs are made **per water source and per licence category**, in accordance with the relevant
  WSP's rules. The process **can only credit accounts — it cannot withdraw water**.

## How an AWD is expressed

**ML per unit share, or percentage of entitlement** — the two are equivalent: "0.5 ML per unit
share, also known as a 50% allocation" credits 0.5 ML to a 1-share licence; **1 ML/share = 100%**.

## Cadence (seasonal — verify against the current Water Allocation Statement)

| When | What happens |
|---|---|
| **1 July** (water-year start) | Opening AWD, deliberately conservative — assessed on near-"worst-case" (~1% probability) inflows. Highest-priority categories (D&S, utilities; then high security) are credited first; general security often opens low. |
| **In season** | Regular assessments (normally **monthly**; the Murray adds a mid-month statement) credit accounts incrementally as inflows exceed minimum forecasts, until categories reach full (100%) allocation. |
| **Event-driven** | Supplementary announcements can be issued at any time (e.g. declared supplementary-flow events). |

## Where published

AWDs and Water Allocation Statements are published by **NSW DCCEEW** on
**water.dcceew.nsw.gov.au** (available-water-determinations and water-allocation-statements pages,
per water source; each WAS states per-category percentages/ML-per-share). Older
industry.nsw.gov.au and water.nsw.gov.au URLs are legacy and redirect. Opening AWDs for the
current water year (2026-27 openings announced 1 Jul 2026) are on that page — verify against the
current-season announcement.
