# NSW — water sharing plans (WSPs) and how to read one

## What a WSP is

A **water sharing plan** is a statutory plan under the WMA 2000 that sets the rules for sharing
water between the environment and users for a specified water source, typically for **10 years**.
**s 20** prescribes core provisions each WSP must contain; **s 21** the additional provisions it may
contain (including water-account operation, carryover of credits and the maximum account credit).
WSPs are published as statutory instruments on legislation.nsw.gov.au. A plan can be **extended
past its 10-year term** (s 43A: Minister, on Natural Resources Commission recommendation, by
gazette notice) or **replaced** where the NRC review finds changes are warranted.

## Status of the 2016 inland regulated-river plans (as at Jul 2026)

The 2016 inland regulated-river WSPs were due to cease 30 June 2026; following the NRC 2024-25
reviews (which recommended replacement), all six were instead **extended two years — now in force
with cease date June 2028** — while replacement plans are drafted (per the DCCEEW region status
pages):

| Plan (2016) | Status |
|---|---|
| NSW Murray and Lower Darling Regulated Rivers | In force (two-year extension), cease Jun 2028 |
| Murrumbidgee Regulated River | In force (two-year extension), cease Jun 2028 |
| Lachlan Regulated River | In force (two-year extension), cease Jun 2028 |
| Macquarie-Cudgegong Regulated Rivers | In force (two-year extension), cease Jun 2028 |
| Gwydir Regulated River | In force (amended, two-year extension), cease Jun 2028 |
| Upper Namoi and Lower Namoi Regulated River | In force (two-year extension), cease Jun 2028 |

Newer regulated plans run on their own terms (e.g. Peel Regulated River 2022, cease Jun 2032;
Belubula Regulated River 2025, commenced Aug 2025, cease Jun 2036). All these plans have been
**amended since commencement** — check the plan text on legislation.nsw.gov.au for current clause
numbers.

## What a WSP contains (rule types)

| Rule block | What it does |
|---|---|
| Environmental water rules | Planned environmental water: translucent releases, Environmental Water Allowances (EWA sub-accounts), Water Quality Allowance. |
| Extraction limit | The long-term average annual extraction limit for the source. |
| Licence rules | Rules for granting/amending access licences; category share components. |
| **AWD rules** | How available water determinations are to be made for each category. |
| **Account rules** | Opening allocation, **account limits, carryover, forfeiture**. |
| **Dealing (trading) rules** | Which s 71 dealings are allowed, into/out of which water sources. |

## How to read one for a licence

1. Find the plan for the licence's **water source** (one WSP per source).
2. Identify the licence **category** clauses (share components).
3. Read the **AWD/opening allocation** clauses for that category.
4. Read the **account management** clauses — carryover percentage, account limit, forfeiture.
5. Read the **dealing rules** for what trades are permitted.

Verified clause mappings from the fetched methodology summaries:

- **Lachlan Regulated River WSP 2016** — categories cll 18-28; environmental water cll 26-28;
  opening allocations cll 27, 28, 44-46, 48, 55.
- **Macquarie-Cudgegong Regulated River WSP 2016** — categories cll 21-25 (D&S cl 21, local water
  utility cl 22, high security cl 23, general security cl 24, supplementary cl 25); EWA cl 14;
  opening allocation cll 34-38; flood-mitigation-zone account withdrawal cl 44(3).
