# NSW — carryover and account limits by regulated valley

- **Carryover** = unused account water brought forward from 30 June into 1 July of the next water
  year.
- **Account limit** = the maximum credit an account may hold (the crediting cap), set by each WSP
  under the s 21 power.

Both **vary by valley and by category** — they are plan rules, not statewide rules. Carryover
applies chiefly to **general security**; in most valleys domestic and stock, utilities, high
security and supplementary licences are re-credited to 100% at 1 July and cannot carry over.
The 2016 regulated-river WSPs that set these rules were extended to June 2028 (see
`nsw-water-sharing-plans`); the rules below remain the in-force plan rules, but the plans have
been amended since commencement — confirm clause text before advising.

## Verified per-valley rules (general security unless noted)

| Valley | Carryover limit | Account limit | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **NSW Murray** | **50%** of entitlement (0.5 ML/unit share, WSP cl 43(2)(a)) | **110%** (1.1 ML/share) — AWD credits + carryover <= 1.1 ML/unit (WSP cl 42(6), cl 34(3)); usage incl. water traded out | Carryover set aside first at 1 July; carryover that would push the account above 110% as allocations rise is forfeited and replaced with allocation water. |
| **NSW Lower Darling** | **50%** of entitlement (0.5 ML/unit share, WSP cl 43(2)(b)) | Effectively **100%** — GS AWD max is **1 ML/unit** (not 1.1); a year's AWD credit is the greater of 0.5 ML/unit or (1 ML/unit minus carryover) (WSP cl 42(7)) | Carried-over GS water is eroded by **Menindee Lakes evaporation and spill debits** (WSP cl 44). D&S, LWU, high security, conveyance and supplementary cannot carry over. Same 2016 WSP as NSW Murray — full text in library `nsw-murray-lower-darling-wsp-2016`. |
| **Murrumbidgee** | **30%** of entitlement | **100%** (carryover + AWDs <= 100%) | Same 30%/100% applies to Murrumbidgee Irrigation conveyance licences. |
| **Namoi — Upper** | **50%** of entitlement | Total account balance <= **150%** | D&S, LWU, high security and supplementary cannot carry over. |
| **Namoi — Lower** | Remaining balances may be carried over | Total account balance <= **200%** | Same category exclusions. |
| **Gwydir** | **Continuous accounting**: hold up to **1.5 ML per share** (carryover limit = account limit = 1.5 ML/share) | 1.5 ML/share; **no maximum AWD** | Annual usage + assignments-out <= 3 ML/share (+ assignments-in); 3-year rolling usage cap of 3 ML/share. Floodplain harvesting: 5 ML/share. All other categories: 100%, no carryover. |
| **Macquarie-Cudgegong** | Carryover via GS/HS/EWA **sub-accounts**; GS carryover cap **1 ML/share** (WSP cl 44 — confirmed from plan text for Cudgegong GS; Macquarie GS same clause, verify) | See notes | During flood-mitigation-zone releases, carryover is proportionally withdrawn and accounts reset to 100% (WSP cl 44). |
| **Lachlan** | Carryover exists and can be large (fetched example: 48% current allocation + 71% average carryover = 119% total GS availability); **numeric cap not verified** | Not verified | Conveyance/EWA/WQA balances are forfeited at year end. |

## Seasonal caveat

Account headroom interacts with rising in-season AWDs (carryover can be forfeited as allocations
climb toward the account limit). **Verify against the current Water Allocation Statement for the
valley and the WSP clause text before advising.**

## Gaps

- **Lachlan** GS carryover % and account limit remain unverified from a primary source (secondary
  guides report an account-style limit of ~200% of entitlement — do not advise on that figure
  without confirming in the Lachlan Regulated River WSP 2016 text).
- **Macquarie** GS carryover cap: cl 44 confirmed as the carryover/sub-account-limit clause and
  1 ML/share confirmed for Cudgegong GS from plan text; the Macquarie GS figure itself was not
  read verbatim.
- **NSW Murray and Lower Darling are now confirmed from the WSP clause text** (cl 34, 42, 43, 44) —
  see library `nsw-murray-lower-darling-wsp-2016`.
- Murrumbidgee/Namoi figures are from Water Allocation Statement narratives (Murrumbidgee 30%/100%
  re-verified Jul 2026), which restate the WSP rules; the Murrumbidgee/Namoi WSP clause text itself
  was not fetched.
