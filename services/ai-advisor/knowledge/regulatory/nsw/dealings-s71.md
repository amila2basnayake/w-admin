# NSW — access licence dealings (the s 71 series)

Water trades in NSW are "dealings" under the WMA 2000. The two everyday trades:

| Trade | Dealing | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Temporary trade** | **s 71T — assignment of water allocation** ("allocation assignment") | Transfers this year's **account water** from one WAL account to another; the share component (entitlement) is unaffected. |
| **Permanent trade** | **s 71Q — assignment of share components** ("share assignment") | Transfers part or all of a licence's **share component** (the entitlement) to another licence. |

The NSW Murray factsheet frames it exactly this way: temporary trade = buy this year's allocation
(s 71T); permanent trade = buy additional permanent shares (s 71Q). In the Murray, allocation
assignments are managed by WaterNSW and share assignments by the department.

## Other dealing types (verified from the fetched WaterNSW dealings material)

| Section | Dealing |
|---|---|
| s 71M | Transfer of the access licence itself (change of ownership of the whole WAL) |
| s 71N | Term transfer (lease-like transfer of entitlements for a period) |
| s 71O | Conversion of a WAL to a new category or subcategory |
| s 71P | Subdivision (cancel a WAL, create two or more) or consolidation (merge WALs) |
| s 71R | Amendment of the share component to a different water source / water management area |
| s 71S | Amendment of the extraction component (limits, rates, circumstances, locations of take) |
| **s 71W** | Nominate/withdraw nomination of a water supply work — also the mechanism for **intrastate and interstate tagging** (linking a WAL to a works approval at another location) |

## Approval requirements

Dealings are assessed against three layers:

1. The **WMA 2000** itself;
2. The **Access Licence Dealing Principles Order 2004** — still the in-force order (not remade),
   most recently amended by the Access Licence Dealing Principles Amendment Order (No 2) 2025
   (2025-703, published 15 Dec 2025);
3. The **dealing rules in the relevant water sharing plan** — water-source specific (some trades
   are prohibited into high-instream-value or stressed sources).

## Water sharing plan dealing rules — worked example (NSW Murray & Lower Darling)

The WSP dealing rules (layer 3) can bar or condition a dealing by **direction and location of
extraction**, not just by category. In the NSW Murray & Lower Darling Regulated Rivers WSP 2016
(library `nsw-murray-lower-darling-wsp-2016`):

- **s 71O** conversion and **s 71R** change-of-water-source are prohibited outright (cl 48, 50).
- **s 71Q** permanent share assignment is barred where it would move the share to a licence
  nominating a work **below the Barmah Choke from one above it** (cl 49(1)(a)); a same-side
  assignment is not caught. Two named licences (conveyance WAL9422, high security WAL9423) are
  carved out of s 71Q and interstate s 71V.
- **s 71T** allocation assignment above→below the Choke needs the Minister satisfied downstream
  supply is not diminished; assignment **into** a supplementary licence (and out of one) is barred
  (cl 51).
- **s 71W** re-nomination cannot move an above-Choke licence to a below-Choke work (cl 53(a)).

See `cross-barmah-choke` for the point-of-extraction framing (a share trade moves the share; the
Choke rules bite on where water is extracted).

## Lodgment and processing (WaterNSW — verify current-season details)

- Allocation assignments (71T) are lodged with **WaterNSW** on the Form 71 for the water type
  (regulated / unregulated / groundwater), emailed to water.trade@waternsw.com.au; account
  balances and trades are visible in **iWAS** (iwas.waternsw.com.au, the Online Water Accounting
  System). In the Murray, allocation assignments are managed by WaterNSW and share assignments by
  the department (DCCEEW).
- **Fees**: $66.75 (GST free) per allocation-assignment application. From **1 Oct 2025**,
  groundwater 71T dealings that need a hydrological impact assessment are referred to NSW DCCEEW
  with an additional referral charge, collected by WaterNSW ($443.61 GST free as listed Jul 2026 —
  verify current fee schedule).
- **Published processing KPIs**: 90% of intrastate allocation trades within 5 business days; 90%
  of interstate allocation trades within 10 business days (see the WaterNSW trade-statistics
  page); share assignments (71Q) take materially longer.
- **Season close (seasonal)**: the trade season closes before 30 June and WaterNSW will not
  backdate or amend an allocation-trade application after the close date. For 2025-26,
  applications were required 10 business days before the end of the water year; 2026-27 close
  dates are announced by WaterNSW — verify against the current-season announcement.

## Murrumbidgee IVT (seasonal)

Murrumbidgee-Murray allocation assignments run through the **Murrumbidgee IVT account**
(limits 0-100 GL). Trade out of (or into) the valley opens and closes with the account balance,
published live (with SMS notifications) on the WaterNSW Murrumbidgee IVT page. Applications are
queued in order of receipt when a limit is hit; queued applications are returned if they cannot be
processed within **7 days of trade closing**. Verify open/closed status before advising.
