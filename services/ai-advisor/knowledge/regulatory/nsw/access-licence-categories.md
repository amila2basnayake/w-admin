# NSW — access licence categories and priority order

**s 57** of the WMA 2000 establishes the categories of water access licence, including regulated
river (high security), regulated river (general security), regulated river (supplementary water),
local water utility, major utility, domestic and stock, and conveyance.

## Priority order (per ss 58 and 60)

s 58 is "Priorities between different categories of licence"; s 60 "Rules of distribution
applicable to making of available water determinations" applies the s 58 priorities to AWDs.

From the fetched High Security Fact Sheet 4: high-security licences "have priority over regulated
river (general security) and regulated river (supplementary water) access licences, but a lower
priority than local water utility, major utility and domestic and stock licences". "Higher priority
means that, if water allocations have to be reduced, the [accounts of] higher priority licence are
to be reduced at a lesser rate."

| Priority | Category |
|---|---|
| 1 (highest) | Domestic and stock; local water utility; major utility (town water) |
| 2 | Regulated river (high security) |
| 3 | Regulated river (general security) |
| 4 | Regulated river (supplementary water) |

## Category profiles

| Category | Profile |
|---|---|
| **High security** | Allocated (up to ~97-100%) before general security; receives full allocation in all but severe drought. Used for permanent plantings, high-value crops, industry, towns. |
| **General security** | Available water varies year to year with climate; the main irrigation category; low/variable reliability; the category most affected by AWD movements and carryover rules. |
| **Supplementary water** | "Uncontrolled flow" that cannot be regulated or stored. Accounts are typically credited 100% (1 ML/share) at the start of the year, but water can only be taken during **declared supplementary-flow events**. |
| **Conveyance** | Issued to irrigation corporations to deliver water through their channel systems; its allocation is a function of high- and general-security allocations (Murray) or e.g. the Jemalong general-security balance (Lachlan). |
| **Town water / D&S / utility** | Highest priority; normally 100% allocation at the start of the year "whenever possible", topped up to full before any general-security allocation. |

## Dividing available water

Each licence category is allocated water **up to a maximum of 1 ML per share — the equivalent of
100% allocation for that category** (NSW Murray sharing factsheet). Priority determines the order
in which categories reach that maximum as the season's resource assessment improves.

WSPs restate these categories as plan clauses (e.g. Lachlan WSP 2016 cll 18-28;
Macquarie-Cudgegong WSP 2016 cll 21-25).
