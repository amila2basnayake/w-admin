# NSW Murray & Lower Darling — trading, allocation and carryover by water class

Covers the two water sources in the **NSW Murray & Lower Darling Regulated Rivers WSP 2016**
(full text: library `nsw-murray-lower-darling-wsp-2016`). Seasonal figures (AWDs, Choke/IVT
balances) must be checked against the current WaterNSW / MDBA announcements — the clauses below are
the **rules**, not a given season's numbers.

## Get the terms right (Water Act 2007 s 4; WMA 2000 s 56)

- **Water access entitlement** = the perpetual share (in NSW, the access licence **share
  component**). **Water allocation** = the seasonal volume credited to that entitlement.
- An NSW access licence has a **share component** and an **extraction component** (WMA 2000 s 56);
  the physical **point of extraction** is fixed by the **nominated water supply work** (s 71W).
- A **share (permanent) trade** moves the share quantum; the **Barmah Choke rules bite on the point
  of extraction**, not on the share itself.

## Allocation (AWD) and carryover by class

| Class | Opening / seasonal AWD | Carryover |
|---|---|---|
| Domestic & stock | 100% of share (cl 35) | None (cl 43(1)) |
| Local water utility | 100% of share (cl 36) | None (cl 43(1)) |
| High security — Murray (unit-share) | 0.97 ML/unit, or 1 ML/unit if GS AWD >=0.97; 0.03 top-up before GS reaches 0.97 (cl 37(2)-(3)) | None (cl 43(1)) |
| High security — Murray subcategories (Aboriginal cultural, community & education, environmental, research, town water supply) | 100% of share (cl 37(1)) | None |
| High security — Lower Darling | 1 ML/unit (cl 37(4)) | None |
| Conveyance (Murray only) | Sliding scale tied to the GS AWD (cl 38 — see below) | None (cl 43(1)) |
| General security — Murray | Up to **1.1 ML/unit** over the year; assessed 1 July + at least monthly (cl 34(3), 39) | **0.5 ML/unit** (cl 43(2)(a)); account cap 1.1 ML/unit incl. carryover (cl 42(6)) |
| General security — Lower Darling | Up to 1 ML/unit (cl 39(3)) | **0.5 ML/unit** (cl 43(2)(b)); year's AWD credit = greater of 0.5 or (1 − carryover) (cl 42(7)); carryover eroded by Menindee evaporation/spill debits (cl 44) |
| Supplementary | 1 ML/unit unless reduced under cl 33 (cl 40); take only during announced events (cl 46-47) | None (cl 43(1)) |

## Conveyance in relative terms (cl 38, cl 23 note)

Conveyance (Murray, 330,000 unit shares; none in Lower Darling) exists to cover delivery/conveyance
losses that grow as GS allocations rise, so its AWD is a **function of the GS AWD**, not an
independent announcement:
- 0.5 ML/unit when HS is at 0.97 and GS at 0;
- +0.003883 ML/unit per 0.01 of GS AWD while GS < 0.6;
- 0.733 base +0.006675 ML/unit per 0.01 while 0.6 <= GS < 1;
- 1.0 ML/unit once GS reaches 1 ML/unit.

## Uncontrolled-flow access for GS (cl 45)

A top-up channel for **general security when allocations are low**, not an AWD credit. The Minister
may authorise GS holders to take uncontrolled flows (not credited to the account) whenever the sum
of GS AWDs is **<= 0.6 ML/unit** and supplementary take is on in the same segment. Capped at
**1 ML/unit minus carryover minus AWD credits** that year; excess is debited back (cl 45(2)-(3)).
Market effect: it shaves peak temporary-market demand/price in dry years for irrigators who can
physically capture the flow, but it is episodic and conditional, so it adds volatility rather than
removing demand.

## Trade rules (Part 9) — permanent (share) vs temporary (allocation)

Trades are prohibited by **direction and location**, so the *permitted* space matters:

- **Category conversion (s 71O, cl 48)** and **change of water source of a share (s 71R, cl 50)** —
  prohibited outright.
- **Permanent share assignment (s 71Q, cl 49)** — permitted within the source **except** where it
  would move the share to a licence nominating a work **below the Barmah Choke from one above it**
  (cl 49(1)(a)); same-side assignment is fine. Conveyance **WAL9422** and high security **WAL9423**
  are carved out of s 71Q (and interstate s 71V, cl 52(3)(a)).
- **Temporary allocation assignment (s 71T, cl 51)** — above-Choke → above-Choke is effectively
  always available; only **above → below** is conditional (Minister satisfied downstream supply not
  diminished). Standing bars: cannot assign allocation **into or out of** a supplementary licence;
  LWU → Lower Darling blocked at Menindee thresholds (below); physically un-suppliable;
  environmental reduction; against IVT Procedures / Schedule D.
- **Tagging / work nomination (s 71W, cl 53)** — cannot re-nominate an above-Choke licence to a
  below-Choke work; interstate/inter-source nomination only per Schedule D + IVT Procedures.

## Barmah Choke — permanent vs allocation (see `cross-barmah-choke`)

- **Permanent (point of extraction):** you **cannot** permanently relocate an entitlement's point of
  extraction **above → below** the Choke, except to reverse a prior below→above relocation recorded
  in the Barmah Choke entitlement record (Schedule D Processing Protocol 2026 cl 11; NSW WSP cl
  49(1)(a), 53(a)). **Below → above is not prohibited** — it is recorded and can offset later back
  trade; note this follows from the *absence of a prohibition* plus the accounting machinery, not an
  express permitting clause.
- **Allocation (temporary):** governed by the Barmah Choke allocation account (reset to zero each
  1 July) and the **no-net-trade rule** — net above→below only under an MDBA **exceptional
  circumstances** determination; **back trade** (reversing prior below→above) is exempt.

## Interstate / IVT limits

Allocation trade in the southern connected system is 1:1, but each receiving system's trade
balance/IVT account is capped, and **trade *in* closes once the cap is hit**, reopening as the
balance falls:
- Trade **upstream into NSW** (from SA, or below→above the Choke) is gated by the Barmah balance and
  IVT accounts — once full, you cannot trade in until deliveries pull it back.
- Trade **into a Victorian zone at its limit** is closed — e.g. the **Goulburn-to-Murray 200 GL**
  limit closes Goulburn → Vic Murray/NSW/SA trade when >200 GL is owed to the Murray.
- Distinct NSW→VIC cap: net **200 GL/year**, or the <50% Murray spill-risk volume, whichever is
  lesser.

## Grandfathered tags (still relevant)

The Basin Plan **s 12.23(2) *exemption*** that let grandfathered tagged entitlements avoid
allocation trade restrictions was removed on **1 July 2024**; the **grandfathered tagged accounts
themselves still exist and remain recognised**, now subject to the same restrictions (with a
reserved Barmah Choke volume, reported ~15 GL — verify). See `cross-interstate-trade-mechanics`.

## Menindee Lakes thresholds (Lower Darling)

The **480 GL / 640 GL** storage triggers gate Lower Darling allocation trade (LWU → Lower Darling
under cl 51), the Lower Darling EWA and restart allowance, and whether Lower Darling storage counts
as "available" for Murray GS (cl 39(4)).

## Market read: GS drives volume and demand

- **General security is the deepest pool by volume** (~1.67M unit shares Murray vs ~190k high
  security), so it carries most of the valley's turnover, permanent and temporary.
- **The GS AWD level is the main driver of temporary-market activity and price** — low/late AWDs =
  scarcity, stronger buy-side demand and higher allocation prices; high AWDs = surplus, more
  sellers, softer prices and carryover competition at 30 June. The wide Murray GS swing (up to
  1.1 ML/unit + 0.5 carryover) generates the year-to-year volatility.
- **Conveyance demand is derived** (tracks the GS AWD, cl 38), not an independent pull.
- **High security trades thinly on allocation** (near-100% reliable); its value shows more in
  permanent entitlement pricing.
