# Victoria — water shares, allocation vs entitlement, HRWS vs LRWS

## Entitlement vs allocation

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Water share (entitlement)** | A legally recognised, **perpetual** entitlement to a secure share of the water available from a declared water system. Traded via a **water share transfer** (change of ownership, change of location, or both), recorded on the Victorian Water Register. |
| **Seasonal allocation** | The water actually **credited to the allocation account** = the seasonal-determination percentage applied to the water share volume. Example: a 50% determination on a 100 ML water share = **50 ML** available to use/trade; a 100% determination = the full 100 ML. |

## Reliability: HRWS vs LRWS

Reliability describes the frequency/likelihood of receiving a full allocation.

| Type | Definition | Sequencing |
|---|---|---|
| **High-reliability water share (HRWS)** | A secure entitlement to a defined share of water, classified high reliability. | **Allocations are made to high-reliability shares first.** Reach high/full allocations with high frequency. |
| **Low-reliability water share (LRWS)** | A water share with a relatively low reliability of supply. | On most GMW systems (all northern Victorian systems **except Broken and Ovens**), LRWS receive a share only once there is enough water to meet higher-reliability shares in the current year **and** — with minimum inflows — the following year. In dry years LRWS commonly sit at **0%**. |

Each seasonal-determination announcement divides the water available for a high (or low) reliability
allocation by the total volume of high (or low) reliability shares in that system, expressed as a
percentage (see `vic-seasonal-determinations`).

## Notes

- Historical (neither limit is current): the **4% annual limit** on water-share trade out of
  northern-Victorian irrigation districts ended **30 June 2014**; the **10% cap on disassociated
  water shares** (shares held without land in a system) was removed by Parliament **16 September
  2009**.

## Seasonal caveat

The allocation percentage on any share is a seasonal figure that opens low each 1 July and climbs.
**Verify the current HRWS/LRWS determination against the live announcement.**
