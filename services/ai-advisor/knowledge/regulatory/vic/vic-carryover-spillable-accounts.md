# Victoria — carryover and spillable water accounts

## Carryover

**Carryover** is an arrangement that lets a water entitlement holder take **unused allocation** from
one season into the next to use and/or trade. In Victoria the season's usable **allocation** =
new-season seasonal determination **plus carryover**.

### Carryover limits by system

| System group | Systems | Carryover rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Large systems** | Murray, Goulburn, Campaspe (plus bulk/environmental entitlements in Wimmera-Mallee) | Carry over unused allocation **up to the full water share (entitlement) volume**. May hold **more than 100%** in the new season when there is space in the dams — the excess sits in a spillable water account. |
| **Small systems** | Broken, Loddon, Bullarook, Werribee | **"100 per cent rule"**: carryover **plus** new-season allocation **cannot exceed 100%** of entitlement volume. Cannot store more than entitlement. |

Carryover is expressed against the holder's **combined high- and low-reliability water share
volume**.

## Spillable water accounts

- **Spillable water** = any carryover or allocation **above** the holder's water share volume.
- Water is moved to the **spillable water account** when carryover plus the current-season
  determination is **greater than the total high- and low-reliability water share volume** (i.e.
  above 100% of share volume).
- **You cannot use or trade spillable water** until a **low-risk-of-spill declaration** is made.
  The statutory declaration-maker is the **Storage Manager** (appointed under Water Act s 122ZK);
  Water Register carryover pages and NVRM announcements refer to the **Resource Manager** — in
  northern Victoria GMW holds both roles. GMW quantifies the threshold as the **risk of spill in
  your system falling below 10 per cent**.
- Spillable water **spills** to make room for inflows/new allocations if the dams fill. Deductions
  from spillable accounts occur when Lake Hume, Lake Eildon or Lake Eppalock spill, or when water is
  released to keep storages near full. (Example: ~292,000 ML was deducted from Murray spillable
  accounts in 2021/22.)

## Seasonal caveat

Whether spillable water is usable, and how much can be carried, depends on live storage conditions
and the current low-risk-of-spill declaration. **Verify against the current-season announcement**
before relying on spillable water for a trade.
