# Victoria — seasonal determinations (northern Victoria)

## What a seasonal determination is

A **seasonal determination** specifies the water available to holders of water shares (and
supply-by-agreement / bulk entitlements), announced as a **percentage of the entitlement (water
share) volume** under current resource conditions.

Since **1 July 2012** the Resource Manager uses "seasonal determination" instead of the former
"seasonal allocation", to distinguish water available under current resource conditions from water
a holder already has via **carryover**.

## Who makes them, and for which systems

- In northern Victoria, **Goulburn-Murray Water (GMW) is the Resource Manager** (appointed under
  Water Act **s 64GA**), trading as the **Northern Victoria Resource Manager (NVRM)**.
- Declared systems covered: **Broken, Bullarook, Campaspe, Goulburn, Loddon, Murray, Ovens**.

## Cadence

- The water year (season) starts **1 July**, with an opening determination announced that day.
- Determinations are updated **fortnightly — on or about the 1st and 15th of each month — from
  August to May**. Interim **risk-of-spill updates** for individual systems may also be published
  between determinations. Treat exact publication dates as system-driven, not fixed statutory dates.

## High- vs low-reliability sequencing

Each announcement divides the water available for a high- (or low-) reliability allocation by the
total volume of high- (or low-) reliability shares in that system, expressed as a percentage.
**High-reliability shares are met first**; low-reliability shares receive allocation only once
high-reliability shares are satisfied (see `vic-water-shares-reliability`).

These are a point-in-time snapshot only. Allocations open low (often 0%) each 1 July and climb
through the season. **Always verify the current percentage against the live NVRM / Victorian Water
Register announcement.**
