# Victoria — trading zones and the Goulburn-to-Murray transfer limit

The consolidated **Water Trading Rules for Declared Water Systems** current version is dated
**1 July 2024** (replacing the 2022 version). Trading zones are declared by Order under Water Act
1989 **s 6B**, made 24 October 2023 and gazetted in Victoria Government Gazette **No. S 613**
(20 November 2023).

## Trading zones (confirmed from the Water Register allocation-trading page)

| Zone | Name | Location |
|---|---|---|
| **1A** | Greater Goulburn | Goulburn system |
| **6** | VIC Murray — Dartmouth to Barmah | **Above** the Barmah Choke |
| **7** | VIC Murray — Barmah to SA | **Below** the Barmah Choke, to the SA border |
| **10** | (referenced with zone 6 for trade through the Barmah Choke) | — |

## Inter-zone trade rule

The trading rules generally permit trade of allocation from one trading zone to other zones
**provided the traded water can readily flow to the destination zone**. **Back-trade** (trade in the
opposite direction) is permitted where a previous forward trade has opened up an opportunity.

## Goulburn-to-Murray inter-valley transfer (IVT) — the 200 GL cap

- The **IVT account** tracks the cumulative water **owed to the Murray** system; the **200 GL IVT
  limit** caps what can be owed. Tagged-account use is subject to the same 200 GL limit as
  allocation trades.
- The **long-term Goulburn-to-Murray trade rule commenced 1 July 2022** (replacing interim rules
  that ran 1 July 2021 - 30 June 2022). It imposes an **annual limit on net trade** from the
  **Goulburn, Campaspe, Loddon or Broken** to the Victorian Murray and interstate.
- Trade opportunity is released via **three annual announcements: 1 July, 15 October and
  15 December**. Around **150 GL** of opportunity is expected annually under average conditions
  (a season-dependent figure — verify against the current-season announcement).
- **Key change from the pre-2022 rule:** delivery of water from the Goulburn IVT account **no
  longer opens up new trade opportunity** — river operators can deliver IVT water without that
  delivery creating headroom for more out-trade. Back-trade (Murray-to-Goulburn) remains permitted.
- Paired **operating rules for the lower Goulburn River** (also from 1 July 2022) require lower,
  more variable flows over summer and autumn.

## Trade openings — how above-cap opportunity is released

The scheduled trade openings for the **Barmah** limit (jointly managed by Victoria and NSW) and
**Goulburn-to-Murray** work as follows:

- A **seven-hour submission window** at the start of the opening; timing within the window gives no
  advantage.
- After close, eligible applications are **randomised** (each gets a random number, sorted, then
  processed in that order); the **ACCC observes and audits** the process. Processing typically
  starts the next day, against the volume available at processing time.
- **Since July 2025, "Trade Remaining Balance" applications are no longer eligible for
  randomisation** (reversing the October/December 2024 practice).

## Barmah Choke limit (a DIFFERENT 200 GL figure — do not conflate)

Trade **through the Barmah Choke** (from zones 6 or 10) is checked against the **Barmah Choke trade
limit** (published via the MDBA — note the MDBA now frames the constraint as the **"Barmah
Narrows"**). **Allocation trade from NSW to Victoria is limited to a net annual volume of 200 GL,
or the volume that keeps the risk of spill in the Murray system below 50%, whichever is the
lesser.** The 2026-27 opening downstream-trade balance was approximately **24 GL** (seasonal —
verify against the current-season announcement). See `cross-barmah-choke`.

Two separate 200 GL figures exist:
- **Goulburn->Murray IVT cap** — the IVT account is capped at **200 GL owed** to the Murray;
  opportunity within it is released via the annual announcements above.
- **Barmah Choke NSW->VIC net annual limit** — **200 GL, or the <50%-spill-risk volume, whichever is
  lesser**.

## Tagged entitlements

- **Long-term tagged-use restrictions have applied since 30 November 2021**: take against a tagged
  entitlement is blocked when the relevant allocation trade limits are reached — tags cannot be
  used to bypass IVT/Choke limits.
- The **grandfathered-tag exemption** (Basin Plan s 12.23(2)) was **removed 1 July 2024**.
- The **Lower Broken Creek exemption** ran only to **30 June 2026 and has now expired**.

## Permanent (water-share) trade out of a district — there is NO annual percentage cap

This is a common and important error: **there is no current annual limit on the volume of permanent
water shares that may be traded out of a Victorian irrigation district.**

- The former **4% annual limit** on water-share trade **out of a northern-Victorian irrigation
  district** was **abolished on 1 July 2014** (its last day of operation was 30 June 2014), across
  **all** districts at once. Do NOT describe it as current, "still live in some zones", or as having
  been raised to a higher rate.
- It was a **Victoria-only** transitional measure that applied at the level of an **irrigation
  district** (an irrigation infrastructure operator's footprint), **not** a trading zone, and it was
  **never a Basin Plan rule** — Basin Plan 2012 Chapter 12 contains no 4% figure and no volumetric
  annual trade-out cap.
- The only residual constraints on permanently exiting an irrigation network are the operator's
  **transformation** process and **termination fees**, governed by the **Water Market Rules 2009**
  (an operator must not prevent or unreasonably delay transformation) and the **Water Charge Rules**
  (which cap the termination fee), enforced by the ACCC / Inspector-General of Water Compliance.
  These shape the **pace and cost** of exit, not a percentage of volume.
- Do **not** conflate this dead 4% district cap with the **live** limits above (the Goulburn-to-Murray
  IVT 200 GL cap and the Barmah Choke / NSW->VIC net annual limit) — those are allocation / inter-valley
  transfer limits, not permanent-entitlement out-of-district caps.

## Seasonal caveat

Trade limits open and close through the season as their net limits are reached. **Verify the current
IVT-account balance and Barmah Choke/Narrows available opportunity against the live figures before
advising on a cross-valley or cross-Choke trade** — Water Register "Trade opportunities" dashboard:
https://waterregister.vic.gov.au/water-trading/market-insights/trade-opportunities (updated
overnight), plus the MDBA site for the Choke.
