# Water Act 1989 (Vic) — unbundled entitlement framework

The Water Act 1989 (Vic) (Act No. 80/1989, in force) is the legal framework for rights to water and
water entitlements in Victoria. It underpins all trading in the Victorian Murray-Darling systems.

## Unbundling

In declared water systems, the former "water rights" and "take and use licences" were **unbundled**
into three separate, independently tradable elements:

| Element | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Water share** | A legally recognised, **perpetual** entitlement to a secure share of the water available from a declared water system. Classified **high-reliability** or **low-reliability**. |
| **Delivery share** | Entitlement to have water delivered to land in an irrigation area. **Tied to the land** and stays with the property on sale. |
| **Water-use licence / registration** | A water-use **licence** authorises use of water for irrigation; a water-use **registration** authorises use for purposes other than irrigation. |

Because the share (ownership/entitlement) is separate from seasonal allocation, delivery and use,
each element trades independently — a permanent **water share transfer** vs a temporary
**allocation trade** (see `vic-water-shares-reliability` and `vic-trading-zones-inter-valley-transfer`).

A separate layer of **"place of take approvals"** took effect 20 November 2023, clarifying the right
to have water delivered separately from water shares across the declared systems.

### Delivery shares — trading and exit

- **Transfer rules (GMW):** delivery shares may be transferred only **within the same delivery
  system**; a buyer cap of **0.1 ML/day per hectare** applies; **no temporary transfers**.
- **Termination:** exiting a delivery share attracts a **termination fee capped at 10x the annual
  access fee** under the Commonwealth Water Charge Rules 2010.
- Delivery share arrangements in northern Victoria have been the subject of a **DEECA Water
  Delivery Share Review** — verify current arrangements against the DEECA review page before
  advising on delivery share changes.

## Allocation account

All water shares must be linked to an **allocation account**. One account can link multiple water
shares, water-use licences or registrations. The account records the allocation made to a share
through the year — the water available to use or trade, including new allocations and carryover.

## Roles

| Party | Role under the Act |
|---|---|
| **Minister for Water** | Issues water entitlements; declares rights in regulated systems; grants bulk entitlements and environmental entitlements. |
| **Water corporations** | Manage regulated/unregulated systems and administer entitlements. In northern Victoria this is Goulburn-Murray Water (GMW). |
| **Resource Manager** | Makes seasonal determinations for declared systems (appointed under Water Act **s 64GA**). |
| **Storage Manager** | Appointed under **s 122ZK**; makes low-risk-of-spill declarations (see `vic-carryover-spillable-accounts`). |

- **Bulk entitlement** = a right to use and supply water in a waterway, in a water corporation's
  storage works, and groundwater; held by authorities such as water corporations.
- **Environmental entitlements** are held by agencies including the Victorian Environmental Water
  Holder and the Commonwealth Environmental Water Holder.
