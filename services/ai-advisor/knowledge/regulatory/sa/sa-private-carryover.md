# SA — private carryover on the River Murray

**Private carryover** lets a Class 3 (High Security) entitlement holder store unused allocation from
one water-use year for use in the next. Unlike NSW/Victoria, SA carryover is a **drought-management
mechanism that is only available in some years** — it depends on a trigger.

## Legal basis

- Rests on **SA's storage (deferral) right under clause 91 and Schedule G of the Murray-Darling
  Basin Agreement 2008**: SA defers/stores part of its Entitlement in upstream River Murray storages
  and can later deliver it for Critical Human Water Needs and/or private carryover in dry years.
- The Minister makes the carryover volume available under **s 127(8) of the Landscape South
  Australia Act 2019**.
- Rules are WAP **s 5.5.2, Principles 62-74**.

## The rules (seasonal — verify against the current-season allocation statement)

| Rule | Detail | WAP reference |
|---|---|---|
| **Availability trigger** | Carryover only granted when the forecast **minimum opening allocation is <= 0.5 kL per unit share (50%)**. Announced on/around **15 April**. | Principle 67 |
| **Maximum carryover** | Up to **0.2 kL per unit share (20% of Class 3 entitlement)**. Carryover volume = unused allocation + quarantined carryover volume, capped at 0.2. | Principle 72 |
| **Account cap** | Current-year allocation + carryover **must not exceed 1 kL per unit share (100%)**. | Principle 69 |
| **Quarantining** | Carryover in excess of the cap is quarantined and can roll forward until a minimum-opening-allocation announcement exceeds 0.5 kL/unit share. | Principles 70-71 |
| **Eligibility** | Must have held an SA water access entitlement on **30 June** of the prior year; metered take with final readings to DEW by **31 July**. **Not available** for the Metropolitan Adelaide pool, All Purpose Classes 1/2/5, Class 8 (ELMA), or the Wetland/Environmental pools. | Principles 62-66 |
| **Traded-in water** | Available carryover may include allocations traded into SA between **1 April and 30 June** that remained unused on 30 June. | Principle 68(b) |
| **Storage loss** | A **5% storage loss** deduction applies to stored carryover. | s 5.5.2 |
| **No guarantee** | Depends on stored volume, spills, evaporation and CHWN demand; if insufficient, holders receive a proportional share. | Principle 74 |

## Worked seasonal example

For 2026-27 the projected minimum opening allocation was 62% (> 50%, announced 15 April 2026), so
the trigger was **not** met and no unused 2025-26 allocation could be carried into 2026-27 —
confirmed in DEW's June 2026 opening-allocation announcement. This changes year to year — check
the ~15 April statement.
