# SA — River Murray Water Allocation Plan (WAP) and allocation announcements

## The instrument

The **Water Allocation Plan for the South Australian River Murray Prescribed Watercourse** ("River
Murray WAP") is made under the **Landscape South Australia Act 2019** (which replaced the NRM Act
2004 from 1 July 2020). The current plan was **adopted by the Minister for Climate, Environment and
Water on 27 April 2023** (still in force as at July 2026; its amendments over the prior plan relate
to the Class 3 private-carryover policy). It covers the River Murray downstream of the Victorian border, including
Lakes Alexandrina and Albert, part of Currency Creek, and the Finniss, Angas and Bremer Rivers.

A WAP sets the water available to take, how it is allocated via **water access entitlements**, the
trade rules, and permitted activities, and must provide for the needs of the environment.

## Consumptive pools (WAP s 2.3.7, Chapter 5)

Entitlements are expressed in **unit shares; 1 unit share = 1 kL**. Full allocation = **1 kL per
unit share = 100%**.

| Pool | Content | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **All Purpose** | Consolidated former Classes 1, 2, 3a, 3b, 4, 5, 7, 8; holds Classes 1, 2, **3 (High Security)**, 5, 8 | **Class 3 (High Security)** = the consolidated irrigation-plus class, total **607,798,212 unit shares** (irrigation-specific former Class 3a = 543,969,767). |
| **Metropolitan Adelaide** | Urban water (former Class 6) | **130,000,000 unit shares**; **cannot be traded**; first 150 GL of SA's Entitlement is Critical Human Water Needs. |
| **Wetland** and **Environmental** | From former Class 9 | — |

Total across all pools (WAP Table 4): **870,134,430 unit shares (~876 GL)**, measured as at
30 April 2018.

## Allocation announcements (seasonal — verify against current-season statement)

- Water year = **1 July to 30 June**.
- The **Department for Environment and Water (DEW)** issues "South Australia's River Murray Water
  Allocation Statement", under the SA River Murray Water Allocation Framework and the WAP, having
  regard to the **MDBA's "worst-case" water resource assessment**.
- Allocation applies to **Class 3 (High Security)** entitlements, expressed as a **percentage**
  (kL per unit share).
- Cadence: projection statements pre-season (wet 10%-likelihood through extreme-dry 99%-likelihood
  scenarios); the **actual opening allocation is provided ~15 June**; from **1 July** updates are
  **fortnightly while allocations are below 100%**.
- Example (2026-27): projected minimum ("worst case") opening Class 3 allocation of 62% (announced
  15 April 2026); the **actual opening allocation of 83%** was announced 15 June 2026 for use from
  1 July 2026, after June rain improved inflows. **Point-in-time example only — verify against the
  current statement.**
- Weekly River Murray flow reports are published via **WaterConnect** (waterconnect.sa.gov.au).
- SA-relevant storages: Dartmouth Dam, Hume Dam, Lake Victoria, Menindee Lakes (MDBA can access
  Menindee above 640 GL until it falls below 480 GL, after which NSW manages it).

## Gaps

- The statements are issued by DEW; the exact statutory division between the Minister and DEW in
  "announcing" the opening allocation is not named in the statements themselves.
- WAP Table 14's per-class figures are mis-aligned in the published PDF layout; per-pool figures
  cited here are from Table 4.
