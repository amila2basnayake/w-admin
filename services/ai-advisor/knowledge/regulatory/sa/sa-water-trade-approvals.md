# SA — water trade approvals on the River Murray

## Instrument

**Chapter 7 of the River Murray WAP — "Transfers of Water Access Entitlements and Water
Allocations"** governs trade of water licences, water access entitlements and water allocations
within the River Murray Prescribed Watercourse, or under an **Interstate Water Entitlements
Transfer Scheme (IWETS)**. The trade rules are consistent with **Chapter 12 of the Basin Plan** and
further the Basin Plan **s 5.07** trade objectives.

## What a transfer can be

- Change of **ownership** and/or change of **location**; whole or part of a holding.
- **Temporary (limited)** — allocation trade — or **permanent (absolute)** — entitlement trade.
- With or without payment.

## Restrictions (WAP Principles 128-131)

| Restriction | Detail |
|---|---|
| Tradeable classes | DEW's trade pages list **Classes 1, 3 and 5** as the tradeable SA River Murray classes. |
| Untradable classes | **Class 8 (ELMA)** and **Metropolitan Adelaide pool** entitlements cannot be transferred (metro allocations only where not contravening the MDB Agreement). |
| Carryover water | Carryover allocations held under Landscape Act s 127(8) **may** be transferred (Principle 129). |
| Ministerial limits | The Minister may restrict trade by location or set a volumetric cap on trade out of an area (Principle 130), with carve-outs for tagged water access entitlements established before 22 October 2010. |

## Who approves

Trades are administered by the **Department for Environment and Water (DEW)** / the Minister under
the **Landscape South Australia Act 2019**. DEW's process pages: "Buying and selling water" and
"Water markets and trade" (see source_urls).

## Process (forms and timeframes)

- Transfer applications use DEW's water licence and permit forms (current **2025/26 editions** at
  the DEW forms page). SA River Murray transfer forms capture **strike date and strike price**
  reporting fields (trade-price reporting).
- **Allocation-transfer guaranteed processing date: the third Friday in June** — applications
  lodged by then are processed within the same water year; later applications are not guaranteed.
- Interstate allocation trades to/from SA: WaterNSW has reported a service standard of **90%
  processed within 20 business days** (vs 10 business days for other interstate trades), but its
  published figures are inconsistent — verify against the current WaterNSW trade-statistics page.

## Interstate trade

- Runs through **IWETS**: entitlements/allocations are issued to IWETS holders, with tagged
  entitlements delivering orders across the border. Trade into SA increases SA's Entitlement.
- Interstate trades require the approval of the water authorities in **both** states, following the
  protocol agreed under the Murray-Darling Basin Agreement (see `cross-interstate-trade-mechanics`);
  interstate trade on the SA side is coordinated by DEW.
