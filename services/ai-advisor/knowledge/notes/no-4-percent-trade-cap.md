Do not state that a 4% annual cap limits permanent trade of water entitlement out of an irrigation district. The 4% annual limit on water-share trade out of northern-Victorian irrigation districts ended 30 June 2014. It was a Victorian rule only, never a Basin Plan or Commonwealth rule, and no equivalent state cap replaced it. What does still constrain leaving a district is the operator-level IIO framework (transformation and termination fees), not a percentage cap.
