# Murray-Darling Basin Agreement (Schedule D – Processing Interstate and Intervalley Trade) Protocol 2026

Source: uploaded file `MDBA (Schedule D _ Processing Interstate and Intervalley Trade).pdf` (legislative instrument (protocol), dated 2026-06-26). Added to the knowledge base 2026-08-24.

## Key points

- This instrument is the Murray-Darling Basin Agreement (Schedule D – Processing Interstate and Intervalley Trade) Protocol 2026, made by the MDBA under clause 6 of Schedule D to the Agreement and section 18E of the Water Act 2007; dated 26 June 2026 and registered 29/06/2026 as F2026L00861.
- The protocol commences on 1 July 2026 (s 2) and applies to interstate transfers and intervalley transfers (s 5).
- Schedule 1 repeals in whole six 2010 Schedule D protocols: Calculating Cap Adjustments, Conversion Factors and Exchange Rates, Permissible Transfers between Trading Zones, Processing Interstate Exchange Rate Transfers, Processing Interstate Transfers of Water Allocations, and Tagging Entitlements for Extraction in another State.
- Clause 11: a water access entitlement may not be transferred from above to below the Barmah Choke, except where it was previously entered in the Barmah Choke entitlement record as having been transferred from below to above the Choke.
- Clause 10: the Authority must maintain the Barmah Choke allocation account, updating the balance in real time or as close as reasonably possible, and publish the balance generally; approval authorities must not approve a transfer that would result in a negative balance.
- Clause 12: for the purposes of clause 12 of Schedule D, the Authority may advise that exceptional circumstances exist for net transfers of allocations from above to below the Barmah Choke, stating the maximum justified volume and any conditions.
- Exceptional circumstances (s 12(2)) exist if the usual challenges in delivering water within channel capacity at the Barmah Choke are significantly reduced.
- The Authority may only find exceptional circumstances if satisfied acting on the advice would not cause material negative impacts on the environmental condition of the Barmah-Millewa Forest or on availability of water to fulfil third parties' water access rights (s 12(3)); it must publish reasons (s 12(5)).
- Clause 13: the Authority must announce for each financial year whether it will consider providing exceptional-circumstances advice, by no later than 31 May before that financial year begins; it may revoke and replace an announcement before the financial year starts.
- Annexure item 17 states the 'no net trade rule': no net transfer of allocations from above or below the Barmah Choke should occur unless the Authority has determined exceptional circumstances apply; item 20 lets State Contracting Governments disapply the rule (other than back trade) when the Authority so advises.
- Annexure item 7: exchange rates will not be applied as a condition of exchange rate trade, and such transfers will not be approved, unless the Inspector-General of Water Compliance has made an applicable declaration under section 12.22 of the Basin Plan.
- Section 14 / Annexure item 11: the Authority and the approval authorities for New South Wales and Victoria are to agree arrangements for processing interstate and intervalley transfers across the Barmah Choke, to be reflected in the procedures.
- State Contracting Governments must notify the Authority and each other of changes to State water transfer law affecting processing (s 9); interstate/intervalley trading zones and conditions will be specified in procedures (Annexure items 2-3).
- Section 101B of the Water Act 2007 prescribes requirements for how a person making a water market decision first announces it (repeated notes).
- The Authority may review and consider amending the protocol on amendment of the Agreement, a clause 15 report, or an independent auditor's recommendation (s 15).

Part 3—Transfers across the Barmah Choke
10  Barmah Choke allocation account
 (1)  The Authority must record relevant information against the Barmah Choke
allocation account for the purposes of sub-clause 11(3) of Schedule D to the
Agreement whenever it is notified that an approval authority or State Contracting
Government approves a water allocation transfer across the Barmah Choke.
 (2)  An approval authority or State Contracting Government must not approve a
transfer if that transfer would result in a negative balance in the Barmah Choke
allocation account.
 (3)  In maintaining the Barmah Choke allocation account the Authority must:
 (a)  establish systems and processes for the purpose of enabling updates to the
balance of the Barmah Choke allocation account in real time, or as close as
reasonably possible to real time; and
 (b)  publish information on the balance of the Barmah Choke allocation
account in a manner that makes it generally available.
11  Restrictions on transfer of water access entitlements
   A water access entitlement may not be transferred from above to below the
Barmah Choke, except where the entitlement has previously been entered in the
Barmah Choke entitlement record as having been transferred from below to
above the Barmah Choke.
12  Exceptional circumstances advice
 (1)  For the purposes of clause 12 of Schedule D to the Agreement, the Authority
may advise in relation to water allocation transfers across the Barmah Choke:
 (a)  that the Authority is satisfied that exceptional circumstances exist, or are
likely to exist, in respect of net transfers of water allocations from above to
below the Barmah Choke for the period; and
 (b)  the maximum volume of transfers that would be justified for the period by
the exceptional circumstances; and
 (c)  any conditions relating to such transfers that the Authority considers would
be appropriate having regard to the exceptional circumstances.

 (2)  Exceptional circumstances exist if the usual challenges in delivering water

Annexure
 (1)  The Contracting Governments have agreed the following principles in seeking to
further the purposes of Schedule D to the Agreement.
Item Description
1 Interstate transfers and intervalley transfers of tradeable water rights may occur in
accordance with rules for interstate transfers and intervalley transfers, including those
specifying trading zones, types of tradeable water rights, and types of transfer permitted
between particular sources as reported by the State Contracting Governments, for the
purposes of Schedule D to the Agreement.
2 Interstate and intervalley trading zones will be specified in procedures. The Authority will
work with Contracting State Governments and the Basin Officials Committee to support
the Basin Officials Committee to define trading zones in the procedures.
3 Interstate transfers and intervalley transfers, including water allocation transfers and tagged
trade, will be permitted subject to conditions imposed by the laws of the relevant State
Contracting Governments. Conditions applying to each trading zone will be specified in
procedures.
4 State Contracting Governments and the Authority will process transfers, including
recording and reconciling information relating to transfers, in accordance with the
protocols and procedures.
5 State Contracting Governments should not approve transfers that are not permitted for the
purposes of the matters agreed to further the purposes of Schedule D to the Agreement.
6 When processing transfers, the Authority, approval authorities and State Contracting
Governments will comply with the procedures.
7 The Contracting Governments affirm that exchange rates will not be applied as a condition
of exchange rate trade, and transfers of this kind will not be approved, unless the
Inspector-General of Water Compliance has made an applicable declaration under
section 12.22 of the Basin Plan.
8 A retailer in a State of Destination may impose and recover such charges as it is entitled to
recover from the person entitled to receive a tagged allocation under the law of the State of
Destination.
9 Bulk water suppliers in a State of Origin should adopt the principle that bulk water charges
be billed in the same way as State water entitlement holders are charged, that is, directly to
the interstate owner of the share as at the date of billing.
10 The Authority, State Contracting Governments and approval authorities will process
transfers relating to the Barmah Choke in accordance with the protocols and procedures.
11 The Authority and the approval authorities for New South Wales and Victoria will agree
on arrangements for processing transfers across the Barmah Choke. This subsequent
agreement will be reflected in the procedures.
12 There will be no transfer of water access entitlements from above to below the Barmah
Choke, except where the entitlement has previously been entered in the Barmah Choke
entitlement record as having been transferred from below to above the Barmah Choke.

Item Description
13 The Authority is responsible for establishing and maintaining the Barmah Choke allocation
account. When recording transfers across the Barmah Choke, the Authority will be
required to:
(a) establish systems and processes for the purpose of enabling updates to the
balance of the Barmah Choke in real time, or as close as reasonably possible
to real time; and
(b) publish information on the balance of the Barmah Choke in a manner that
makes it generally available
14 Approval authorities and State Contracting Governments will not approve a transfer of a
water allocation if that transfer would result in a negative balance in the Barmah Choke
allocation account.
15 When processing transfers relating to the balance of the Barmah Choke, approval
authorities must:
(a) ensure any information published in relation to the balance, or other
announcement that may affect the market for transfers across the Barmah
Choke, accurately reflects any decision made by the Authority that relate to
the balance or such transfers; and
(b) take reasonable steps to ensure the accuracy of transfers recorded that affect
the balance; and
(c) promptly rectify any errors relating to the balance.
16 The Contracting Governments affirm that decisions relevant to transfers made by the
Authority, a State Contracting Government or an approval authority must be made
generally available, and agree that publication and notification requirements may be
prescribed in procedures.
17 No net transfer of water allocations from above or below the Barmah Choke should occur
unless the Authority has made a determination that exceptional circumstances apply or will
apply at the time of the transfer. This is known as the ‘no net trade rule’.
18 The Authority will announce by no later than 31 May each year whether it will consider
making a determination of exceptional circumstances for the following financial year. An
announcement of this kind will be made generally available, and will be subject to any
other publication requirements under the procedures.
19 The Authority may from time to time advise, in accordance with the protocols and
procedures, that exceptional circumstances apply for a period such that net transfers up to a
specified volume would be justified (including any conditions that would be appropriate to
apply to such transfers for this purpose).
20 If the Authority advises that exceptional circumstances apply, the State Contracting
Governments may disapply the no net trade rule for transfers other than back trade.
21 The Contracting Governments:
(a) affirm that any water announcements made by the Authority relating to the
Barmah Choke, including determinations of exceptional circumstances, must
be made generally available; and
(b) agree that additional publication and notification requirements may be
prescribed in procedures.
 (2)  In this Annexure:
bulk water supplier means an authority responsible for recovering costs
associated with operating headworks from which allocations are supplied within
a State of Origin.
