# Water Sharing Plan for the New South Wales Murray and Lower Darling Regulated Rivers Water Sources 2016 [2016-366]

Source: uploaded file `NSW Water Sharing PLan for the New South Wales Murray and Lower Darling Regulated Rivers Water Sources  2016.pdf` (water sharing plan, dated 2025-11-28). Added to the knowledge base 2026-08-24.

## Key points

- Plan made under s50 Water Management Act 2000, commenced 1 July 2016, has effect for 10 years (extendable a further 10 years under s43A); this is the current version for 28 November 2025 and was amended in 2022 to meet Basin Plan/water resource plan commitments.
- Applies to the NSW Murray Regulated River Water Source and the Lower Darling Regulated River Water Source within the Lower Murray-Darling Water Management Area, extending downstream to the South Australian border.
- Available water determinations (Part 6 Div 5): domestic and stock, local water utility, and specified high-security subcategories get 100% of share component; Murray general-security AWDs may total up to 1.1 ML per unit share; Lower Darling high security 1 ML per unit share (cl 34, 35-40).
- Murray regulated river (high security) unit-share licences receive 0.97 ML per unit share, with an additional 0.03 ML per unit share made before general-security AWDs reach or exceed 0.97 ML per unit share (cl 37).
- Carryover (cl 43): no carryover for domestic/stock, local water utility, high security, conveyance or supplementary licences; Murray and Lower Darling general-security licences may carry over up to 0.5 ML per unit share.
- Murray general-security account credits from AWDs in a water year must not exceed 1.1 ML per unit share minus volume carried over; Lower Darling general-security credits capped at greater of 0.5 ML per unit share or 1 ML per unit share minus carryover (cl 42(6)-(7)).
- Barmah Choke trade constraints (Part 9): assignment-of-rights dealings from upstream to downstream of the Barmah Choke in the Murray are prohibited (cl 49); allocation assignments upstream-to-downstream are prohibited unless the Minister is satisfied supply of downstream orders is not diminished (cl 51(a)); nomination of downstream works by upstream licences prohibited (cl 53).
- Conversion of licence category (s71O, cl 48) and change-of-water-source share dealings (s71R, cl 50) are prohibited; interstate transfers (s71U) require administrative arrangements and Schedule D rules apply (cl 52).
- Allocation assignments involving conveyance licence WAL9422 or high-security licence WAL9423 are prohibited, as are inter-source assignments except to/from the Murrumbidgee Regulated River or the two plan water sources (cl 49, 51, 52).
- Lower Darling allocation-in dealings from a local water utility licence are prohibited when Menindee Lakes storage is below 480,000 ML or has not risen above 640,000 ML since last falling below 480,000 ML; interstate allocation applications after 30 April in a water year are prohibited (cl 51(c), 52(3)(d)).
- Long-term average annual extraction limit: Murray is the lesser of specified extraction calculations minus 17,800 ML/year; Lower Darling minus 35,500 ML/year; non-compliance triggered at 3% or more exceedance (cl 27, 29).
- Long-term average sustainable diversion limits subtract 262,000 ML/year (Murray) and 8,000 ML/year (Lower Darling) under Basin Plan Schedule 2/3 (cl 30).
- Environmental water allowances (Part 10): Barmah-Millewa Allowance capped at 350,000 ML, Barmah-Millewa Overdraw capped at 50,000 ML, Murray Additional Allowance capped at 0.15 ML per unit share, Lower Darling Allowance held at 30,000 ML when Menindee conditions met, plus a 60,000 ML Lower Darling River Flow Restart Allowance (cl 55-64, 72).
- Commencement share component estimates include Murray general security 1,674,096 unit shares, Murray high security 189,704 unit shares, Murray conveyance 330,000 unit shares, and Lower Darling general security 79,507 unit shares (cl 22-25).
- Supplementary water events cannot be announced in various circumstances (e.g. Morgan salinity above 800 EC units, active waterbird breeding, or if Lower Darling flows needed to keep Darling River at 7,000 ML/day at Weir 32) (cl 47).
- Assessed channel capacity constraints include Barmah Choke 8,500 ML/day, Tocumwal Choke 10,600 ML/day, Hume Dam and Lake Mulwala 25,000 ML/day, Edward River/Kolety offtake 1,600 ML/day, Gulpa Creek 350 ML/day, Pooncarie 20,000 ML/day (cl 67).

7 Vision statement
The vision for this Plan is to provide for the following—
(a) the health and enhancement of the water sources and their water-dependent ecosystems,

(d) social and cultural benefits to urban and rural communities that depend on surface water.
