Source: uploaded file `Terms and Conditions of Use of the Waterfind System 01072025 (2).pdf` (terms and conditions / user agreement, dated 2025-07-01). Added to the knowledge base 2026-08-26.

## Key points

- The Waterfind System is operated by Water Find Pty Ltd (ACN 105 979 664); the terms are version-dated 01072025 and reference number F01958564.
- Waterfind acts solely as an intermediary/execution-only service, gives no advice or recommendations, and is not personally liable in respect of any Waterfind Transaction (clauses 2.5, 31.2).
- Waterfind reserves absolute discretion to refuse or terminate registration and to refuse, suspend or terminate any listing, water order, offer or acceptance without reason (clause 2.2).
- Users are solely responsible for password confidentiality and are liable for all unauthorised use of their account; Waterfind may assume any instruction containing the password is valid (clauses 3.2, 3.3).
- Covered transaction types are Permanent Transfer, Temporary Transfer, Carryover Facility, Long Term Lease and Water Options (including assignment) (clause 38.1.66).
- Purchasers in a non-forward Temporary Transfer must pay the price plus GST, estimated government/water authority fees and the Purchaser Conveyancing Fee into the Waterfind Trust Account within 24 hours of acceptance (clause 9.1.1).
- For Permanent Transfers, payments into the trust account are due within 48 hours of acceptance; a Qualifying Permanent Transfer covers private-diverter parcels registered on the NSW (WAL), Queensland (DNR) or Victoria (WEE) titling systems (clauses 9.2, 38.1.36).
- Forward Transactions require the balance of purchase price and estimated fees to be paid not less than 14 days prior to the delivery date (clauses 9.1.2, 9.2.3).
- Fees payable to Waterfind include Purchaser/Vendor Conveyancing Fees, Lessor/Lessee Conveyancing Fees, Waterfind Placer/Holder Fees, Issuer/Taker/Assignee Option Fees and a Failed Transaction Fee if a transaction fails due to a party's act or omission (clauses 8, 10, 12, 14).
- Waterfind is entitled to keep interest earned on monies held in its Trust Accounts and pays that interest to itself (clause 17.2).
- A Notice of Exercise for a Water Option is irrevocable once given, and the obligation to transfer the underlying parcel cannot be cash-settled or closed out (clause 7).
- Information Credits expire 12 months after being awarded or purchased, are redeemed first-awarded-first-redeemed, are non-transferable, non-refundable, have no monetary value and cannot be converted to cash (clauses 23-27).
- Waterfind's liability is limited to the cost of re-supplying its Services, and it excludes liability for system disruptions, execution errors, delays and counterparty breaches to the maximum extent permitted by law (clauses 32.3, 32.4).
- Users indemnify Waterfind (and its officers, directors, agents, employees) on a full indemnity basis and grant Waterfind irrevocable authority/power of attorney to execute transfer and application documents (clauses 18, 33).
- The terms are governed by the laws of South Australia, with non-exclusive jurisdiction of SA courts and the Federal Court of Australia (clause 37.1).
