# AI Water Advisor — Question Suite (50), generated blind

> **What this is.** 50 questions the AI advisor is likely to field, written **outside-in**: generated by
> a fresh Claude instance given **only** the public business description of Waterfind and ten user
> personas — **no** knowledge of the advisor's tools, data, guardrails, or wiring, and explicitly barred
> from reading this repo (it made 0 tool calls). The point is fidelity to real *user intent* rather than
> questions reverse-engineered to match known features. Contrast with `advisor-question-suite.md`, which
> is the insider/capability-mapped version.
>
> **Why keep both.** The insider suite tells you *"does each feature work."* This blind suite tells you
> *"what will actually get typed"* — including messy, emotional, naive, and out-of-scope questions
> (asking about a neighbour, "just do the trade for me," "what will the price do next month"). Several of
> these land outside what the advisor can do; that gap is the signal, not noise.
>
> **Personas used:** small family irrigator · corporate agribusiness finance director · vineyard owner ·
> pastoral station manager · almond orchard manager · pure water investor · Aboriginal corporation water
> officer · brand-new client · Waterfind broker/account manager · cotton/rice grower (northern/mid Basin).
