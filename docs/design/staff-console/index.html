Waterfind — Broker / Staff Console

Five exploratory wireframes for internal broker & staff tooling, sharing one shell: the brand header, the 64px hover-expand left navigation ribbon, and the navy/orange section bars reconstructed from the live client portal. Data is illustrative, drawn from the loaded waterfind-db worked example (Kyle Egan · Njernda, region 515). Reference only — no stack chosen (see repo CLAUDE.md).

01 · Dashboard

Broker Desk

Landing overview — KPIs for your book, watched-market prices & depth, recent settled trades, an action queue, firing alerts, and settlements needing attention.

Open wireframe
02 · Trading

Markets & Order Book

Live bid/ask ladder with depth, price strip, recent settled trades, and a place-order-on-behalf-of-client panel with an estimated-net + match preview. Routes through the real order path.

Open wireframe
03 · CRM

Client 360

One client, whole picture — identity & consent, holdings, tradability reach, trade history with the net-proceeds breakdown, seasonal allocation, and a read-only advisory synthesis.

Open wireframe
04 · Back office

Settlements & Trust

The money trail — settlement pipeline with stage progress, the ISO-9001 trust ledger reconciled to $0, money-flow summary, disputes, and compliance exceptions.

Open wireframe
05 · Bottom of the ribbon

Admin & Settings fully tabbed

The required screen: pinned to the bottom of the left nav ribbon and organised as nine working tabs — Profile, Users & Roles, Fees & Commission, Tradability Rules, Notifications, Tenancy & Access, Integrations, Audit Log, Compliance.
