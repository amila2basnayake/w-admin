Why the guardrails
No DB-level tenant isolation

The database is a single public schema — a naïve read leaks every client. The assistant must replicate the app's EXISTS (… tenant_to_user …) gate on every query, which is why scope can't be turned off.

Auditable by design

Regulated across VIC · NSW · SA + the MDBP with independently-audited trust accounts. Grounding, citations and the action_log trail keep every AI-assisted decision traceable.
