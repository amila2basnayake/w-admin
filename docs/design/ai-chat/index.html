Waterfind AI — Water-Rights Assistant

Five wireframes exploring how a grounded water-rights chat assistant could live inside the broker console — the same patterns you'd A/B in a real product. All reuse the console shell (brand header, the 64px hover-expand left navigation ribbon, navy/orange bars) so the assistant feels like one product with the staff tooling. Answers are grounded in the loaded waterfind-db (Kyle Egan · Njernda, region 515), always cite the table behind a figure, flag estimates & stale data, respect the tenant_to_user scope, and never place a trade themselves. Reference only — no stack chosen (see repo CLAUDE.md).

01 · Bottom of the ribbon

Assistant Workspace fully tabbed

The required screen: a full page pinned to the bottom of the left nav ribbon, visible on login, organised as five working tabs — Ask (the live conversation), Threads, Prompt Library, Knowledge & Sources, and Guardrails.

Open wireframe
02 · Docked panel

Copilot Dock

A context-aware assistant drawer docked to the right of whatever screen you're on. It watches the live order book for region 515, answers in place, and pre-fills a place-order draft you still review & submit.

Open wireframe
03 · Floating widget

Chat Launcher

The familiar bottom-right bubble that pops a compact conversation over any page — quick-start chips, a settlement-status answer with a live progress card, and a typing indicator. Lowest-footprint way to ship first.

Open wireframe
04 · ⌘K overlay

Command Palette

Keyboard-first, spotlight-style ask-anything (⌘K). Returns an inline grounded answer with a net-proceeds card and citations, blended with jump-to-screen and run-a-prompt commands. For power users who live on the keyboard.

Open wireframe
05 · Split canvas

Workspace + Canvas

Two-pane research surface: conversation on the left, a live artifact on the right — a prioritised call list rendered as a table, chart, the exact (tenancy-gated) SQL, and its sources. For deeper, multi-step advisory work.
